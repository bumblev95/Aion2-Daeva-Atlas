The reward and the fight, together

Krao CaveBerk
GLOBAL · CONQUEST

Krao Cave

Entry: Lv 45, total item level 700

TARGET REWARD

Rupture weapons · accessories

Unique · item level 54
DB example: Rupture Claymore

Build your first Conquest weapon; reserve its voucher for a missing slot.

Sources: MetaBot · Krao Cave

KR CONQUEST LESSON

Route / preparationLeft boss → right boss → central Seal Guardian → final room; fly across the gap.

Berk

  • Yellow rocks appear.Reach a rock early; check protection.
  • Red particles surround Berk.Retreat and avoid his chase.
  • The stagger bar unlocks.Use reserved stagger skills together.

DraupnirBakarma
GLOBAL · CONQUEST

Draupnir

Entry: Lv 45, total item level 700

TARGET REWARD

Bakarma armor · guard

Unique · item level 54
DB example: Bakarma Guard

Replace weak armor slots; compare the guard conversion before repeating for one drop.

Sources: MetaBot · Draupnir

KR CONQUEST LESSON

Route / preparationOpen the entrance control, glide left to the first boss, then use updrafts and the activated wind path.

Bakarma

  • Rings spread from the boss.Jump before contact; approach for stagger.
  • Bakarma dives head-first.Observe heading, then the underwater silhouette.
Watch the patterns and movement →

Urugugu CanyonAuldor
GLOBAL · CONQUEST

Urugugu Canyon

Entry: Lv 45, total item level 1,400

TARGET REWARD

Aulamus weapons · accessories

Unique · item level 70
DB example: Aulamus Greatsword

Upgrade the damage side of your set; keep Wrathful materials for crafting or a Market sale.

Sources: MetaBot · Urugugu Canyon

KR CONQUEST LESSON

Route / preparationUpper boss → lower boss → defeat the Wind Mage → follow the wind paths to Auldor.

Auldor

  • Airborne boss; red target marker.Gather on the marked ally.
  • Feathers fly forward.Dodge; if caught, use shock removal.

Vakron’s Floating IslandVakron
GLOBAL · CONQUEST

Vakron’s Floating Island

Entry: Lv 45, total item level 1,400

TARGET REWARD

Vakron armor · guard

Unique · item level 70
DB example: Vakron Guard

Fill armor gaps beside the Urugugu weapon route; target the slot you are still wearing at a lower tier.

Sources: MetaBot · Vakron’s Floating Island

KR CONQUEST LESSON

Route / preparationBreak the rock barrier, defeat the Odyle Rock Spirit and follow the wind route; place four elements at the final altar.

Vakron

  • A player’s health bar turns green.Keep clear, then attack the vines.
  • Stagger locks; the party is pulled in.Break the red rock; use its opening.
  • Red floor; rings and straight attacks.Sidestep lines; jump three rings and the follow-up.
Watch the patterns and movement →

Fire TempleKromede
GLOBAL · CONQUEST

Fire Temple

Entry: Lv 45, total item level 2,100

TARGET REWARD

Enraged Kromede weapons · accessories

Unique · item level 86
DB example: Enraged Kromede Claymore

Keep a weapon you intend to enhance further; Wrathful Ego also feeds high-tier Dragon Lord crafts.

Sources: MetaBot · Fire Temple

KR CONQUEST LESSON

Route / preparationBring cleansing potions for burns. Agree who activates the cooling controls before regrouping at bosses.

Kromede

  • The bow points at the floor.Retreat; expect a repeat at low HP.
  • Kromede moves to the centre.Spread and evade both clone hits.
  • Gapped walls approach.Align with each new gap; watch projectiles.

Fierce Horn CaveNuakum
GLOBAL · CONQUEST

Fierce Horn Cave

Entry: Lv 45, total item level 2,100

TARGET REWARD

Nuakum armor · guard

Unique · item level 86
DB example: Nuakum Guard

Complete the armor side of this tier; compare a voucher conversion with another random drop.

Sources: MetaBot · Fierce Horn Cave

KR CONQUEST LESSON

Route / preparationBomb the wall → first boss → break the left wall → totem → necromancer → updraft to the final boss.

Nuakum

  • A clone targets an immobilised player.An unhit ally blocks; rotate blockers.
  • A circle appears after the clone.Large: target alone. Small: share.
  • Central energy; green-health target.Align boss → energy → target each shot.

After the run

After the clear

Turn the drop into the next upgrade before choosing another run.

Equip the useful drop

Compare the new base item with your enhanced equipment. A higher base tier is a reason to compare, not a reason to discard the old item immediately.

Keep the matching voucher

Save the dungeon-specific voucher for the recipe that fills your missing slot; leave enough Kinah for conversion.

Use or sell crafting drops

Keep inputs for your planned craft. Compare tradable surplus with current Market listings instead of treating every dungeon item as sellable.
