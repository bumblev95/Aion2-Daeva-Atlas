Turn materials into useful stats

Global play reports reviewed here show individual Insight capped at 3; the dated KR guide describes levels up to 5. Use your region’s track.

Souls first; expensive rerolls later

Pick one missing pet or the next Insight level before hunting. Leave when that goal is reached; add another family when it addresses your current stat gap.

Lock only a roll worth keeping

Locked slots raise analysis costs. Decide the stat and a Kinah/crystal limit before rerolling; an automatic grade stop does not guarantee the stat you wanted.

Use a dungeon when souls are the goal

Odylium Repository awards Growth Pet Chests. It shares entries with the stone and Kinah dungeons, so choose it when pets are your next upgrade.
