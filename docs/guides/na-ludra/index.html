NA Ludra: beginner preparation & rewards

Choose a difficulty, prepare your gear and spend the right vouchers. NA combat verification remains open.

NAReviewed 2026-11-04

NA launch guide · official facts and preparation advice are separated. Current combat patterns are not yet verified.

Choose difficulty

Enter with a realistic goal

First visit? Start with Normal

Our recommendation: learn with a practice group on Normal, even if you meet the Hard gate. Normal offers the same reward types; Hard has different drop rates and is intended for highly progressed, coordinated Forces. Neither entry level guarantees a clear.

ModeEntry / limitReward choice
NormalCharacter item level 2,800 · 60 minutesGear Change Voucher: Ludra (Bound)
HardCharacter item level 3,000 · 60 minutesGear Change Voucher: Ludra (Hard) (Bound)

Official NA patch · Sanctuary / Bracelet. The one-hour limit is the run limit, not a verified boss-enrage timer. The gates use your character total, not an individual weapon level.

Below 2,800: improve gear first. At 2,800–2,999: Normal is available by item level. At 3,000+: both gates are met; choose Hard after your group is comfortable with the current fight. Confirm unlocks and remaining entries in the NA client.

Gear & party

Before you spend an entry

Preparation advice · no verified stat minimums

NC has not provided a required gear set, enhancement level, Accuracy target or DPS check. Do not buy a full set just to follow an old KR or Early Access threshold.

  1. Improve the gear you will actually useCheck your weakest slots, useful options and available enhancement budget. Keep a usable combat build while raising the entry total; compare transfer previews before replacing equipment.
  2. Prepare skills and suppliesReview your main skills, Stigma and Arcana bonuses. Put recovery items and defensive skills on accessible keys, and check food/scroll durations in your client. These are preparation suggestions, not confirmed Ludra consumable requirements.
  3. If you need more materialsThe November 4 patch raises Exploration rewards in Draupnir, Vakron Sky Island and Ferocious Horn Den. Refining stones and crafting materials match Normal quantities but are Bound. Check the selected dungeon reward screen before spending entries.
  4. Agree on a practice planChoose a caller, clarify tank/healing responsibilities and allow time for retries within the hour. Before entry, check party requirements, reward eligibility and the displayed re-entry/resurrection rules together.

Combat status

Learn the current NA fight

NA combat patterns: not yet verified

The official return notice promises new mechanics. The November 4 patch confirms entry, rewards and crafting, but does not document attack cues or counters. This review found KR/Early Access guides, not a verifiable post-update NA combat reference. No boss pattern, damage value, HP trigger, enrage time or revival allowance is asserted here.

  1. Before the first pullConfirm the region, difficulty and recording date of any video your group uses. Treat older guides as background only; do not assign orb colours, safe tiles or resurrection tactics from them as NA rules.
  2. During practiceUse the current on-screen warnings and your caller. If the group fails, record the warning, boss HP, player positions and outcome before changing one response at a time. This is a learning method, not a proven counter.

Still open: NA attack sequence and cues, safe positions, difficulty-specific mechanics, resurrection/re-entry rules, entry costs and weekly reward limits. Do not assume unlimited revives or a fixed number of clears to finish a bracelet.

Choose a reward for your next upgrade

Both modes offer a Gear Change Voucher as a guaranteed-reward choice, including the mode-specific Ludra voucher. For a bracelet goal, check that exact name before selecting. The patch gives no per-run voucher count or reward cadence, so 6 vouchers does not mean 6 clears. Different drop rates do not establish which mode is more efficient for your group.

Optional event goal: a Normal or Hard clear during November 4 after maintenance–November 24 before maintenance (PST) qualifies for Ludra Hunt recognition. The universal title is Ludra’s Nemesis; ranked titles are for the first 20 Hard-clear Forces per listed region, including NA East and NA West separately. Do not choose Hard solely for the universal title.
