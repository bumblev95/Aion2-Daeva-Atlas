Which AION 2 class should you play?

Choose by the decisions you enjoy making, then compare your shortlist with a repeatable trial.

Playstyle advice · Global & KR

01

Start with your job, not a letter grade

+

A useful first choice is the responsibility you want in a fight. Templars suit interest in the frontline, Clerics in healing, and Chanters in supporting a party. Damage-oriented choices then split into close-range and ranged styles. These are orientation labels drawn from class identities, not a ranking of current balance. A class can feel comfortable in one activity and demanding in another.

02

Build a two-class shortlist

+

Compare one familiar option with one that genuinely interests you. Gladiator versus Assassin asks whether you prefer sustained frontline involvement or precise engagements. Ranger versus Sorcerer asks whether physical ranged combat or a magic-focused sequence appeals more. Cleric versus Chanter is a question about primary responsibility, rather than which is universally better. Include Spiritmaster if summon management itself sounds enjoyable. Avoid levelling all eight just to postpone a decision.

03

Use the same trial for both

+

Give each candidate a short, comparable session. Use similar progression, equipment and the same available encounter; do not compare a geared main against a fresh alternate. Notice three things: whether you can see important threats, whether the controls feel comfortable, and whether you enjoy the recovery after a mistake. A clean repeated run is more informative than a highlight clip. Record what you disliked as well as what felt powerful.

04

Match the class to your actual week

+

If most sessions are short and solo, test solo comfort first. If you have a regular group, discuss missing responsibilities and schedules before rerolling. For PvP, identify whether you mean duels, small groups or larger fights; those contexts ask different questions. Do not treat an unlabelled PvP tier list as a universal answer. The comparison tool below deliberately uses roles and range rather than unsupported damage scores.

05

What would change this recommendation?

+

Revisit the choice when your preferred activity changes, when a patch substantially alters a mechanic you rely on, or when the controls continue to feel uncomfortable after practice. A small balance change is not by itself a reason to abandon a character. Before following a build from Korea, confirm the skill exists in your client and that its description matches. This guide is editorial decision support; it is not a tested endgame build.
