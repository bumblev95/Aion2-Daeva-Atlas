Bracelet

A raid reward with a purpose

Abyssal Bracelet

Global play guide recipe: 3 Ludra Gear Vouchers (Bound) + 5,000,000 Kina.

Collect raid rewards

Follow the Ludra reward route; keep vouchers for the intended craft.

Arcana

Craft for a skill you use

Base Unique Arcana

Global table: 5 Mysterious Crystals + 2,000,000 Kina. Extra selected options add costs.

Choose the right card

Chalice, Parchment, Compass, Bell and Mirror occupy different slots. Compare the skill/passive bonus before crafting.

Material route

Keep, collect, then craft

  1. Choose the finished itemOur advice: name the slot or skill you want to improve.
  2. Reserve useful cardsCrystals have season reward and Arcana extraction routes; do not extract a card you still need.
  3. Review the full recipeBase crafting and optional picks are different budgets. Keep enough Kinah for the next useful upgrade.
