NA · November 4

Prepare from confirmed NA information

NA announcement: October 23 · rechecked October 25, 2026. The older Global references below keep their original review scope.

  1. Before launch · compare your entry totalUse the announced NA goals: Normal 2,800 / Hard 3,000. Global gear level 102 describes one equipment item; it is neither this character entry total nor a confirmed NA reward level.
  2. Before launch · plan temporary energyNC announces 2 Odyle Energy per character per day by mail, October 28 after maintenance–November 10 before maintenance (PST). Mail expires 7 days after receipt; check regional maintenance notices and the mailbox timer.
  3. Before launch · read Ludra Hunt eligibilityThe first 20 Hard-clear parties per listed region receive ranked titles; other event-period Normal/Hard clears receive Ludra Hunter. Announced window: November 4 after maintenance–November 24 before maintenance (PST). Read NC’s full rules; entry level alone does not guarantee a clear or title eligibility.

Verify in the NA client · entry & events

After launch: difficulty labels, displayed entry gate, party size, unlock requirements, entry/reward limits, clear recognition and title delivery. When support starts: mail reset time, claim conditions and expiry. No direct gameplay verification is claimed.

Verify in the NA client · rewards & combat

Check actual gear levels and bracelet recipes. Attack triggers, counters, enrage/revive rules and drop probabilities are not published in this notice; no NA pattern or drop-rate guide can be derived from it.
