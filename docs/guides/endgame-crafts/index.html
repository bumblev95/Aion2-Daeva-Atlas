NA · November 4, 2026: current official patch

Normal requires character item level 2,800 and Hard requires 3,000. Both have a one-hour limit. The official patch now confirms the launch rewards and bracelet-voucher conversions below.

NA November 4 patch summary → · Current facts / verify in game →

Abyssal Bracelet · NA official

Abyssal Bracelet (Bound) can drop from Normal or Hard and can be Amplified to level 3. The patch does not publish its drop rate.

Ludra's Bracelet · voucher conversions

6 Normal vouchers or 3 Hard vouchers → Ludra's Bracelet (Bound), which can be Amplified to level 5. Two Normal vouchers can be converted into one Hard voucher.

Check the live morph preview

Both difficulties offer guaranteed voucher choices, but reward counts, drop rates and any displayed currency cost still need client verification before spending.

NA · November 4

Prepare from confirmed NA information

NA patch: November 4, 2026. Older Global references retain their original dates and scope.

  1. After launch · compare your entry totalUse the official NA gates: Normal 2,800 / Hard 3,000. Entry level is only a gate and does not guarantee a clear. The patch does not publish boss triggers, counters, enrage or revive rules.
  2. Plan temporary energy by accountThe corrected current term is 4 Odyle Energy per account per day by mail, October 28 after maintenance–November 10 before maintenance (PST). Mail expires 7 days after receipt.
  3. Read Ludra Hunt eligibilityThe first 20 Hard-clear parties per listed region receive ranked titles; other event-period Normal/Hard clears receive a universal clear title. The window is November 4 after maintenance–November 24 before maintenance (PST).

Verify in the NA client · entry & events

After launch: difficulty labels, displayed entry gate, party size, unlock requirements, entry/reward limits, clear recognition and title delivery. When support starts: mail reset time, claim conditions and expiry. No direct gameplay verification is claimed.

Verify in the NA client · rewards & combat

Check reward item levels, the live voucher-morph menu and amplification behavior. Attack triggers, counters, enrage/revive rules and drop probabilities are not published; no NA pattern or drop-rate guide can be derived from the patch.
