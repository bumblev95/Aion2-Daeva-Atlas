Cost & character slots

Base transfer · 0 Quna

Once per account across the whole transfer event, not once per character or per week.

Each moving character · 0 Quna

No fixed character-count cap, but destination slots and eligibility constrain your selection.

Each extra slot · 1,000 Quna

Origin character-slot expansions stay at origin. Available destination slots determine the fee.

6 characters → 4 empty slots → 2 new slots → 2,000 Quna

With enough empty slots, total transfer cost is 0 Quna. Otherwise: max(0, moving characters − empty destination slots) × 1,000 Quna, only if those slots can still be expanded. This is a Quna cost, not a quoted USD price.

Character Storage

Items and purchased storage expansion slots move with that character.

Server Storage

Items and stored Kina do not move. Only expansion records carry over, using the larger of the two servers’ expanded storage-slot counts.

Kina / Abyss Points

Kina in the moving character’s inventory/Character Storage moves in full. Products also marks Abyss Points and Kina/Abyss Point daily earnings as transferred.

Quna / Mileage

The origin-server balances move in full and add to destination balances. They are moved, not copied for characters left behind.

Membership / Daeva Pass

Membership combines at destination and is removed from origin. Daeva Pass retains its existing progress.

Pets & shared progress

Pets, Genus Crystals and skins

Merge at destination; removed at origin. Characters staying at origin can no longer equip the moved pets/skins. Pet level, collection level/rewards also carry over.

Genus Insight choice

Choose origin or destination progress, not a sum of both. If you already have a destination character, compare both before choosing. Origin Genus Insight resets whichever you choose.

Duplicate shop purchases

A duplicate Special Pet gives a Pet Reward Ticket; duplicate non-customizable decorative skins give a Skin (Single) Reward Ticket. Pass Shop duplicates of those types refund Pass Coins.

Pet tickets: request a refund through Customer Support. Skin (Single) tickets: exchange for the Shop’s 300 Quna item. Products gives expiry at Nov 24 PST / Nov 25 CET maintenance; Q&A instead labels November CEST. Confirm the deadline with NC; neither ticket works after expiry.

English Global names are retained where a Korean client equivalent is not verified. This is not the KR/TW transfer policy.

Dungeon rewards & after arrival

Dungeon reward progress is retained

Remaining Cube reward claims and attempts left until guaranteed rewards move, including Expeditions and Transcendence. A move is not a fresh reward allowance.

Character content usage moves

Abyss time, Spacetime Rift time, Abyss Corridor usage and related reward counts carry over. Corridor access still requires destination-server capture.

Server-wide reward progress

Daily Dungeons, Shugo Festival and Dimensional Invasion progress transfers. The Q&A says origin-server reward progress also stays unchanged; do not infer duplicate rewards.

Missions, rankings and shop limits

Season missions/claim history and Supply Requests carry over. Season rank points move and are removed at origin. Character purchase counts carry over; server-limited Coin Shop counts use the higher server count, so transfer does not restore purchases.

During the transfer period, the Q&A says Wind Breeze Merchants’ Soul Crystals and Instant Clear Tickets (Server) shift to equivalent quantities through Substance Morph; Odyle Energy purchase limits change from server-based to character-based. Check your current client before buying.

After transfer: choose a new name (all moving characters get a temporary name, even without a collision). Friends/Blocked lists persist. Transfer processing logs the account out and blocks login until completion; NC lists a minimum of one minute with possible longer queues or temporary restrictions.
