KR Snowfield of Grief: raid movement

Orb assignments, below-50% movement and the October 14 change.

KRReviewed 2026-10-13

Dated source guidance; confirm the material and reward preview for your region.

Orb assignmentsBelow 50%Patch & party
Orb assignments

Keep assignments and rings separate

Oct 9 proposal: four assigned DPS each handle two cold orbs. This is one raid strategy, not a universal party convention.

Below 50%

Bring the boss back safely

Order A

Central ground → large hazards + spin → targeted teleport slash. The guide uses an ally pull after the outside jump.

Order B

Teleport slash → spin → large hazards + central move. Return timing differs; agree before the pull.

Patch & party

Use the current raid rules

October 14 · KR

NC reduces Deltras’s Ice Wedge HP by 50%. Recheck who can break it; the Oct 8 strategy predates this adjustment.

Before joining

Agree assignments, the selected movement order and assistance skills. These KR raid lessons do not establish Global availability.
