Today’s route

Choose today’s money route

A short route works best when it funds one named upgrade.

Need stones and Kinah

Finish duties for Kinah; choose Bio-Research Base for stones. Put both into the equipment goal you already selected.

Have stones; lack enhancement money

Use an available Daily Dungeon entry on the depository. Add unfinished field rewards before paying to redraw more tasks.

Saving for a Market purchase

Set the target purchase price, farm its tradable funding route, and reserve your bound balance for enhancement. Skip analysis rerolls that delay that purchase.

Market sales

What can become Market money?

The drop matters only if that particular item can be traded and someone buys it.

Dungeon crafting materials

Wrathful Mind: the six classic dungeons. Wrathful Will: Urugugu/Vakron and the higher pair. Wrathful Ego: Fire Temple/Ferocious Horn. These feed Dragon Lord crafting; sell only tradable surplus.

Gather along a useful route

Compare ore, wood and Odyle listings before taking a detour. A gathered material kept for your own recipe saves a purchase; only a completed sale creates income.

Price a craft from its inputs

For example, Ruby Powder and Yggdrasil Paper are processed tradable inputs. Compare selling the raw material with processing it; include purchased ingredients and displayed selling fees.

Net proceeds = completed sale − materials bought − selling costs

No live prices or measured Kinah-per-hour are available here. Test a small batch before spending your enhancement budget on stock.
