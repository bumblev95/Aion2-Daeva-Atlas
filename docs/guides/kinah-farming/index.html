Kinah farming & spending

Earn enhancement funds and Market money through the activities that pay each kind.

Official change · NA only · November 18, 2026

All field-monster Kina rewards become Bound Kina. The daily 1,000,000 Kina acquisition limit likewise changes to Bound Kina; this is an acquisition ceiling, not a guaranteed payout. Do not count direct field-monster currency as Market purchasing funds. This NA notice does not establish KR rules.

Still requires in-game checks

The notice does not specify account versus character scope, reset time, HUD counter behavior, whether duties/dungeons/quests share this cap, or what happens to other loot at the cap. It does not enumerate enhancement costs accepting Bound Kina or the debit priority. Check the NA reward counter and payment preview before budgeting; no live test is claimed here.

Official system · NA only · November 25, 2026

After maintenance, Membership users can use a World Market and World Exchange to trade across servers within the same faction and region. The existing Server Market and Server Exchange stay available. World sales have a 20% tax, with separate daily sale-listing limits of 10 for the World Market and 10 for the World Exchange (Quna).

World trading · direct checks pending

The announcement does not state whether each listing limit is per account or character, when it resets, or how cancelled/expired listings count. Verify the post-maintenance menu, Membership gate, same-faction/region scope, displayed tax and settlement, and both counters before changing a farming budget. The 20% figure is for World sales; do not apply it to the retained Server markets without separate evidence.

Sources: NC · November 18 NA currency update · NC · November 25 NA World Market notice

Need stones and Kinah

Finish duties for Kinah; choose Bio-Research Base for stones. For NA, direct field-monster currency is now bound: budget it for enhancement only where the payment preview accepts it, not for Market purchases.

Have stones; lack enhancement money

Use an available Daily Dungeon entry on the depository. Add unfinished field rewards before paying to redraw more tasks.

Saving for a Market purchase

Set the target purchase price and use completed sales of tradable surplus to build Market funds. Direct NA field-monster Kinah does not fill that budget. Reserve bound funds for costs that accept them; skip analysis rerolls that delay the purchase.

Market sales

What can become Market money?

The drop matters only if that particular item can be traded and someone buys it.

The field-currency change does not announce that all material-sale routes are removed. Keep the routes below conditional on the exact item being tradable and the sale settling. Bound variants, including the bound Wrathful materials listed for Ludra, are not sale stock.

From November 25 after maintenance, eligible NA Membership users can compare the retained Server Market with the new World Market. For a World sale, budget from the official 20% sales tax and the displayed settlement amount. The daily World sale counters are limited separately to 10 Market listings and 10 Exchange (Quna) listings; these are listing limits, not promised sales.

Dungeon crafting materials

Wrathful Mind: the six classic dungeons. Wrathful Will: Urugugu/Vakron and the higher pair. Wrathful Ego: Fire Temple/Ferocious Horn. These feed Dragon Lord crafting; sell only tradable surplus.

Gather along a useful route

Compare ore, wood and Odyle listings before taking a detour. A gathered material kept for your own recipe saves a purchase; only a completed sale creates income.

Price a craft from its inputs

For example, Ruby Powder and Yggdrasil Paper are processed tradable inputs. Compare selling the raw material with processing it; include purchased ingredients and displayed selling fees.

Net proceeds = completed sale − materials bought − displayed selling costs

World sales use the announced 20% tax. No live prices or measured Kinah-per-hour are available here. Test a small batch before spending your enhancement budget on stock.
