AION 2: What Does 3b Mean? Three-Boss Runs

3b means clearing all three bosses in an AION 2 party run. Decode 200k+ / 3b / tp, compare run plans and open boss mechanics and reward guides.

QUICK ANSWER

In AION 2 party recruitment, 3b means clearing all three bosses in the run. The party plans to kill bosses 1, 2 and 3 before finishing.

How to read the party listing

RECRUITMENT EXAMPLE

200k+ · 3b · tp

200k+
Combat Power of at least 200,000 in this example. The leader sets the requirement; this is not a recommended Global cutoff.
3b
All three bosses in that dungeon run.
tp
Teleport shorthand. Ask which travel point or route is prepared.

Agree on the run before entering

If the listing says 3b, stay for all three boss kills. A final-boss-only run has a different plan. Check the dungeon, difficulty and route with the leader before joining.

A useful question: “All three bosses, and which teleport point are we using?”

Connect the route to your reward goal

A clear and a reward claim are separate checks. Match the boss drops to the item you want, then check your available entries and reward requirements. The KR source explains why its three-boss route matters for Kinah; it does not establish a fixed payout for every Global run.
