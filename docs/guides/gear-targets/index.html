How far should this item go?

Our order prioritizes a usable set before expensive finishing work.

First: a useful Unique at +10

For ordinary Unique gear, +0 to +10 is guaranteed in the cited Global curve. Build the weapon you will use, then patch armor gaps that block survival or entry.

Then: one long-lived weapon to +15

Above +10, ordinary enhancement can fail and consume stones and Kinah. Reserve the next upgrade’s budget before committing to one weapon.

Finally: amplification and fine rolls

Eligible max-enhanced Unique gear uses Amplify Stones and Kinah. Add expensive Manastone rolls after settling on the base item.

Ordinary failed enhancement loses materials, not levels. Clash Runes can lose a level; amulets and belts use their own scroll track. These rules are not interchangeable.

When the target will not drop

Keep vouchers and compare conversions before discarding spare equipment.

Missing guard or armor

Draupnir, Vakron and Ferocious Horn vouchers have guard and armor-chest conversions. Compare the recipe’s Kinah cost with another run.

Wrong weapon or missing accessory

The weapon-side dungeons have accessory-chest recipes and weapon-selection conversions. A weapon swap also needs the matching dungeon weapon; a voucher alone is not a free weapon.

Planning past this ladder

In this Global snapshot, Ludra weapons/guard and Splendent Wise or Ebony Dragon Lord crafts reach item level 102. Price the recipe chain before buying materials.
