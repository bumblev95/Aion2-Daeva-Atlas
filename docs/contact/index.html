Contact & corrections

Help make the guide more useful with a source-backed correction.

Found an outdated detail, a broken page or a translation that misses the meaning? Send a clear correction through our GitHub issue tracker. This is the site’s current contact channel.

Report a correction ↗

What helps us verify a correction

  1. The guide URL and the sentence that needs changing.
  2. Your game region, the relevant update date and the activity.
  3. A public official source, or a clear description of a reproducible observation.

GitHub requires an account to submit issues, and reports are public. Please do not share credentials, payment details or private screenshots. Game account or billing support belongs with NC, not this fan project.
