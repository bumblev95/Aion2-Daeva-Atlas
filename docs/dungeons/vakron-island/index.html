Hard rewards · guaranteed, chance and achievement
  • Guaranteed dungeon-specific Ariel Fragment: Vakron (Bound).
  • Philosopher’s Stone: Revelation (Bound): random reward opportunity and a guaranteed selectable reward option. Do not assume both are paid on every clear.
  • Expedition Hero Artwork: chance reward. More Kina than Normal; exact amounts and rates are not announced.
  • Unique materials: Wrathful Mind, Wrathful Will and Wrathful Ego can be obtained. Unique Skins require Hard Expedition achievements, not merely a random boss drop.
  • Substance Morph: 100 fragments → 1 Daevanion Crystal: Ariel (Bound), up to 30 times per character; 1 fragment → 5 Enhance Stones, with no conversion-count limit. Menus: Special → Daevanion / Consumables.
Still needs a North America gameplay check

Record the server/date and selected difficulty, entry screen and prerequisites, a full boss attempt, Frenzy start, personal revive counter before/after a wipe and re-entry, and the reward selection screen. Check reward eligibility, energy/entry consumption, quantities, binding and morph limits. Do not infer extra patterns, hit damage or a recommended DPS from the four-minute timer.
