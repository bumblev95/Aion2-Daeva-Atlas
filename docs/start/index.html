ABSOLUTE BEGINNER / 8 SMALL STEPS

Never played an MMO?
Start right here.

You get a task, fight, collect rewards, equip them, and learn skills. Let’s try that loop one click at a time.

Looking for useful settings? Try four practical tips ↓
LESSON 01 / 08

Get one task

A quest is a task from the game. It gives you a destination and a reward.

PRACTICE SCREEN · simplified illustration
◆ EPISODE

Speak to the marked character

EXP +　◇ Quest reward

Your destination is now marked. Follow the selected task.

Now try it in the game

  1. Open the quest menu and choose an Episode quest.
  2. Read the objective, then use its tracking or destination control.
Episode ?

The main story. It unlocks areas and systems.

NPC ?

A character controlled by the game, not another player.

LESSON 02 / 08

Attack, then check your health

The enemy loses HP when you hit it. Your red HP bar is how much damage you can still take.

PRACTICE SCREEN · simplified illustration
♜ENEMY
YOU
HPMP

The enemy lost health. Your MP also fell: some skills spend this resource.

Now try it in the game

  1. Find a quest enemy and select it. Use a skill that is in range.
  2. Watch your HP and MP. Move away from danger before continuing.
HP ?

Reaching zero means your character falls.

MP ?

A resource used by some skills; some attacks restore it.

LESSON 03 / 08

Move out of the red area

A colored area can warn where the next hit will land. Watch the ground as well as the enemy.

PRACTICE SCREEN · simplified illustration
♜ENEMY
YOU
HPMP

You moved before the attack landed. Now attack again.

Now try it in the game

  1. Practise moving sideways while keeping the enemy visible.
  2. Find your dodge and jump bindings in control settings. Some boss patterns need a jump, not a dodge.
Telegraph ?

A warning of an incoming attack. Not every attack has one.

Cooldown ?

The wait before you can press the same skill again.

LESSON 04 / 08

Finish and collect the reward

Doing the task and collecting its reward are separate steps. The next quest may wait for that reward.

PRACTICE SCREEN · simplified illustration
✦EXP◇GEAR◎KINAH

EXP grows your character level. Gear goes into your inventory.

Now try it in the game

  1. Return to the marked destination or open the completed quest.
  2. Confirm the reward and check that the next Episode objective appears.
EXP ?

Progress toward your next character level.

Kinah ?

The game’s ordinary money. You use it for several upgrades.

LESSON 05 / 08

Put the new item on

An item sitting in your bag does not strengthen you. Equipment works when it is in its character slot.

PRACTICE SCREEN · simplified illustration
INVENTORY⚔→EQUIPPED□

The weapon moved to the equipped slot. Check its attack and requirements before replacing the old one.

Now try it in the game

  1. Open Inventory and select the quest reward.
  2. Compare the same equipment slot, check class restrictions, then choose Equip.
Item level ?

A number describing equipment, separate from your character level.

Enhancement ?

Spending materials to improve an item you own.

LESSON 06 / 08

Spend a skill point

A skill is an action. A skill point raises that skill. Your character level and the skill’s level are different.

PRACTICE SCREEN · simplified illustration
✦Chosen skillLv. 1Available points 1

Skill Lv. 1 → 2. The point was spent on this skill, not on your character level.

Now try it in the game

  1. Open Skills. Pick the class below to see your first two priorities.
  2. Select that skill, check the cost and increase its level when allowed.
Passive ?

A learned effect that works without pressing an attack button.

Stigma ?

An extra skill you choose and equip after the system unlocks.

LESSON 07 / 08

Keep a way back

A Kibelisk is a travel point. Find and activate it in the game as your quest takes you past it.

PRACTICE SCREEN · simplified illustration
◇⌖◇

KIBELISK

The stop is checked in this practice. Use the map guide to find real locations.

Now try it in the game

  1. Choose Elyos or Asmodian and your current region in our map.
  2. Select a Kibelisk marker, find it on your in-game map and interact with it.
Faction ?

Elyos and Asmodians begin in different regions.

Sealed Dungeon ?

A field activity with permanent-growth materials. Read its entry and reward screen.

LESSON 08 / 08

Enter your first party carefully

A dungeon is a separate combat area. Party members survive by responding to boss rules together.

PRACTICE SCREEN · simplified illustration
♜✚⚔✦➶

FIRST RUN WELCOME

Tell the party it is your first run. Watch one boss pattern before entering.

Now try it in the game

  1. Read the dungeon difficulty and entry requirement; choose a group that welcomes first runs.
  2. Open our boss guide: watch the cue, where you move, and what ends the attack.
Tank / healer / damage ?

Roles: manage enemy attention, restore allies, or deal damage. Everyone still avoids mechanics.

Stagger ?

A boss disruption system. Some phases require skills that damage its stagger gauge.
