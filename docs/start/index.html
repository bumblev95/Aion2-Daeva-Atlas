WHEN YOU GET STUCK

What is stopping you?

My next quest is locked

Read the exact requirement in the journal. Check a missing prerequisite, level or progression condition before farming random enemies. Mark the required objective on the map.

I keep dying in a dungeon

Replay the moment before the hit. Was it a floor attack, control effect or party mechanic? Learn that cue, then practise the response. Gear alone may not solve it.

Watch boss mechanics →
There are too many upgrade systems

Separate your character level, equipped gear, skill levels, Stigma choices and Daevanion board. Pick the system tied to your current bottleneck; a higher item score does not explain all of them.
