DAMAGE LAB / CONDITIONAL MODEL

Build the rotation.
See the damage.

eDPS uses the whole fight, including movement and invulnerability. Change damage and cooldowns to compare patch scenarios.

The initial values are selected level-1 Global tooltip terms. Cast time is an editable 1-second assumption. This is not an optimized final build or a verified class tier list.

Checking the current patch…

When can the skill be used?

Burst Arrow needs Slow or Root. Condemnation needs Chain of Torment. Blaze needs Fire Mark. Judgment and Dark Crush need a preceding skill. Missing conditions are never assumed to be active.

Effects are created at action completion and checked at the next cast start. Windows end exclusively. Chance-based hits/procs, an unknown trigger duration, and KR effects need your confirmed windows. Fire Mark’s 20% extra-damage proc is separate and excluded. Chain of Torment’s 10s DoT is not used as a verified Condemnation window.

Enter confirmed state / trigger windows

Seconds from fight start, e.g. 0-5, 20-25. Empty means no confirmed window. Only enter immunity-target procs when the relevant skill actually became available; its tooltip percentage alone is insufficient.

MP budget & element stacks

MP is spent at cast start, gained at completion, and regenerates during downtime. A blank skill cost is unknown and blocks that skill when MP limits are enabled. Do not interpret a source dash as zero. Elemental Fusion needs four stacks and consumes all four; spirit attack frequency is not guessed. Stamina costs are unverified; Insignia scaling requires an explicit consumption assumption. These values are inputs, not measured stats.

Class condition audit & sources
Periodic damage, Insignia and target inputs

Known damage terms are separate from unknown timing rules. Defaults omit DoT and Insignia scaling. Enable only the assumptions you want to test; they are not live-game verification.

Edit per-tick damage, interval, lifetime and first tick in the skill row. Tick time keeps running during no-damage windows; suppressed ticks are lost and ticks after fight end are excluded. Less than 100% hit chance needs a verified application timeline, so automatic DoT/Insignia application is blocked. Savage Roar gives one 10s Insignia; the model caps at five and assumes each stack expires separately. Its refresh/consumption rules remain unverified. NPC chance never bypasses immunity.

Skill inputs & priority

Higher rows are used first when ready. Direct-hit terms and periodic ticks are separate. Enter measured timings; summons, trap triggers and charge durations require your inputs. Cooldowns start at cast start; casts crossing a no-damage window are skipped.

Known direct terms are retained. Unresolved charge/summon/trap rows stay blocked until you confirm your damage and timing inputs.

Comparisons stay in this browser. Load a saved build to revise it after a patch; export CSV for a separate copy.

Formula, assumptions & what is missing

Expected hit = (base + attack × coefficient) × hits × (1 + crit chance × (crit multiplier − 1)) × hit chance × user multiplier

eDPS = total expected damage / full fight seconds

Defense, amplification stacking, proc damage, cooldown resets, buff bonuses, hit-specific critical rules and skill-level scaling are not inferred. DoT first-tick, critical and refresh rules and Insignia consumption are explicit input assumptions. Known activation gates and optional MP budgets are checked separately from expected damage. Fold only known effects into your input; avoid counting them twice. The patch percentage changes the modeled skill’s damage, not the whole class.

An endgame ranking still needs patch-matched final skill levels, specializations, Arcana, Daevanion, gear, rotation timings and repeatable combat evidence for every class. Those datasets are currently incomplete.
