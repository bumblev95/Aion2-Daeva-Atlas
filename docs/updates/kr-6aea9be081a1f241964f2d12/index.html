    Deltras · abnormal Frozen Bind damage

    Fixes an intermittent condition that applied abnormal damage to a target caught by Frozen Bind during Deltras.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: this is a damage-behavior recovery without a published value. Reproduce the same Bind sequence before considering the encounter stable; no buff, nerf or DPS percentage is assigned.

  • • Other change

    Maintenance compensation

    A bound Special Supply Chest was claimable November 4 15:30–November 5 15:30 KST and contained 3 Life Crystals, 2 Revival Spirit Stones, 3 Combat Enhancement Scrolls and 2 Small Odyle Energy. The chest expires November 11 at 05:00.

    Combat impact P2 analysis: this is a temporary KR recovery item, not a permanent growth route or NA reward.
