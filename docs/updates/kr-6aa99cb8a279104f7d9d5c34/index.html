FULL CHANGE LIST

Everything else in this patch

01Rift occupation

  • Monolith contribution rises substantially. Occupation participants cannot phase into wanted missions/legion airships or travel to another territory.

02Abyss

  • Abyss monsters drop unbound heroic and unique Abyss potential stones.

03Shugo Festa · race track

  • Obstacles and objects that disrupted normal racing are repositioned.

04Snowfield raid

  • Easy-mode returned Triton and Deltras have 10% less HP.

05New gear

  • True Guardian/Archon captain weapons and guards arrive through the Abyss store or inheritance crafting with earlier captain equipment.

06Abyss gear set bonuses

  • The 12-piece PvE Abyss amplification increases. The table retains every resulting set value.

  • Some contribution medals increase for Abyss bosses, artifact battles and Rift occupation.

  • Odium Storage pet-box rewards double; mission/order Soul Crystals triple.
  • Special-shop Soul Crystals cost 500 Kina with a 30,000-unit purchase cap.

09Manastones & spiritstones

  • Nightmare/Festa stores add higher-grade bound stones; emergency supply orders add lower-grade stones.

10Abyss invitation pass

  • The subscription menu offers basic access for 1,000,000 Kina and premium for 500 Quna once per character; premium also supplies five bound Od Energy.
  • Sales run September 16 maintenance–October 6 05:00; missions/claims end at October 7 maintenance. The level-40 cap has repeating rewards; a level costs 50 Quna.
  • Weekly XP is capped at 12,000 per character. All XP sources and level rewards appear below; pass coins are spent in the subscription pass store.

11Quality of life

  • A new alliance leader occupies the first slot of party one. Guard damage to spirits is aligned with damage to players.

12Other fixes

  • Mistimed cooldown resets cannot grant unintended double casts. Exposed columns/kisk structures in Eltnen part two are hidden.
  • Deltras fixes cover the post-sword ice wall, frozen-target selection, stun after resurrection, transition damage spikes and fixation remaining after reset.
  • Triton fixes cover early crystal explosions during stagger, Cold Fury stagger locking/execution and Cold Harvest orbs disappearing early or causing sudden boss death.
  • Purple Winter Core crystals gain colorblind readability without hitbox changes. Trial commendation popups, gauntlet resale prices and the Black Agent sunglasses design are corrected.
