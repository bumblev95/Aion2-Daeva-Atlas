PATCH NOTES / KR

November 11 · Korea

KR raises the cap to 55, restructures dungeons, Abyss schedules and weekly routes, and reworks every class around new buffs, cooldowns and conditional chains. These rules apply with the November 11 maintenance and are not copied to North America.

CLASS CHANGES

Gladiator ↔ Adjustment

Adjustment 1

Class guide →

    Frenzy and core attacks

    Several PvE attacks gain 20–100% damage, Ruinous Blow gains 50% base damage and grants Frenzy, while Downward/Upward Strike lose about 50% base damage and become Frenzy-gated. Zikel’s Blessing changes from 120s to 60s.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: burst setup and finisher timing change materially, but the patch does not establish total rotation DPS or a final tier.

CLASS CHANGES

Templar ↔ Adjustment

Adjustment 1

Class guide →
  • ↔ Adjustment

    Judgment rotation and defenses

    Judgment PvE damage falls 10%, cooldown changes 5s→3s and it gains up to three uses plus consecutive-use bonuses. Shield Smite changes 10s→7s, while Warding Strike and Doom Shield change 30s→45s; Punishment and Retributive Strike also lose PvE damage.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: frequent Judgment chains trade against lower individual damage and longer defensive cooldowns; reproduce charges, reset order and threat.

CLASS CHANGES

Assassin ↔ Adjustment

Adjustment 1

Class guide →
  • ↔ Adjustment

    Insignia resource and burst

    Insignia becomes a self buff lasting 15s. Insignia Explosion changes 10s→5s, costs 100 MP and scales from 110% at one stack to 500% at five; Evasion Stance changes 20s→10s but lasts 1s and heals 5% on specialization 4.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: stack generation and five-stack burst become the main reproduction target; exact animation timings and sustained DPS remain unmeasured.

CLASS CHANGES

Ranger ↔ Adjustment

Adjustment 1

Class guide →
  • ↔ Adjustment

    Precision and Resonance loop

    Rapid Fire and Aimed Arrow lose 25% and 10% PvE damage. Rupture Arrow gains 10%, creates Resonance under Precision and deals 150% extra damage; Explosive Arrow gains 80% PvE damage and its follow-ups move to Resonance. Drill Dart changes 5s→4s and Bow of Blessing 120s→60s.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: Precision→Resonance sequencing is now central. Reproduce buff acquisition, reset chance and Arrow Barrage activation before changing a build guide.

CLASS CHANGES

Sorcerer ↔ Adjustment

Adjustment 1 · Bug fix 1

Class guide →
  • ↔ Adjustment

    Cold/Fire summons and Winter burst

    Winter’s Shackles gains 250% PvE and 5% PvP damage with revised Winter Shard stacks. Cold Storm and Fire Wall change 60s→45s, ignore combat-speed/cooldown-reduction effects on summon attacks and become mobile; Fire Wall summon-skill cooldown is halved.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: published gains coexist with timing-rule changes. Summon tick timing, stack order and mobile-cast behavior must be reproduced before an eDPS claim.

  • ✓ Bug fix

    Other Sorcerers’ enhanced debuffs

    Fixes Cold Storm and Fire Wall dealing extra damage to targets carrying another Sorcerer’s Enhanced Frostbite or Enhanced Embers.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: unintended cross-player damage is removed. No arbitrary nerf percentage is assigned; test one- and two-Sorcerer parties under matched conditions.

CLASS CHANGES

Spiritmaster ↔ Adjustment

Adjustment 1

Class guide →
  • ↔ Adjustment

    Spirit Descent and Elemental Fusion

    Normal-spirit cooldowns change 15s→30s. Elemental Fusion loses 35% PvE damage but gains 100% PvE/50% PvP during Spirit Descent. Spirit Descent becomes all-element amplification (5.3% at level 1, +0.3% per level) and lasts 6s for every summon.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: the class shifts toward short Spirit Descent windows. Pet attack cadence and delayed-damage application remain required inputs, so total eDPS is not inferred.

Cleric ↔ Adjustment

Adjustment 1

Class guide →

    Damage skills and Condemnation gate

    Multiple PvE attacks gain 15–40%. Condemnation gains 15% PvE, loses 15% PvP and activates for 2s after Debilitating Mark or 3s after Chain of Torment instead of requiring the target’s Chain-of-Torment state. Light of Protection level-1 PvE/PvP values change 10.5%/5.25%→5.5%/2.75%.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: the damage chain is easier to schedule but defensive amplification is lower. Reproduce activation windows, specializations and healing trade-offs.

    Chants and new Storm Order

    Undefeated Mantra level-1 PvE/PvP amplification and resistance change 10.5%/5.25%→5.5%/2.75%. Sprinting Mantra is replaced by Storm Order: 10s of 5% boss damage amplification at level 1, +0.5% per level, while healing amplification becomes 0%.

CLASS CHANGES

All classes ↔ Adjustment

Adjustment 1 · Other change 5

  • ↔ Adjustment

    Groggy finisher specialization

    Specialization 5 now reduces Mastery-skill cooldowns by 0.5s instead of reducing every skill cooldown by 1s.

    BeforeAll skill cooldowns −1s→AfterMastery skill cooldowns −0.5s

    Combat impact P1 analysis: cooldown routing narrows to Mastery skills; reproduce the affected skill list and rotation timing in the KR client.

  • • Other change

    Level 55 and new regions

    The KR cap rises to 55 and Eltnen/Morheim part 2 opens. Large Hidden Cubes in four regions consume 10 keys for 10× rewards.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: map and beginner routes need new-zone prerequisites and verified cube coordinates, spawn/acquisition conditions and reset behavior.

  • • Other change

    Expedition and Transcendence restructure

    KR adds level-55 Urugugu Expedition (800K/1.000M CP) and Eracus Transcendence (800K/1.000M/1.200M CP), removes several older modes and changes entry gates from item level to Combat Power. Many modes support solo scaling; Eracus final-boss kills are capped at five per week.

    Combat impact P0/P1 analysis: old KR dungeon gates and routes can block or misdirect players. Verify unlocks, party scaling, boss mechanics, reward eligibility, pity sharing and Wednesday 05:00 reset.

  • • Other change

    Abyss schedule and rules

    Season 5 splits into two halves. Surface entry keeps level 45 and item level 1000 while removing upper limits, applies equalized stats/skills, cuts weekly time 7h→3h (subscription 14h→6h), and reduces AP by 30%. Artifact groups move to 22:00/22:30/23:00.

    Combat impact P0 analysis: current schedule and entry guidance must use the new KR rules. Verify server matching, equalization, consumable restrictions, AP cap overflow and boss spawns in game.

  • • Other change

    Awakening Battle and growth route

    Awakening Battle becomes regular Growth Dungeon content, changes weekly attempts from 3 to 9 per server and adds instant completion after one clear with a ticket. A new four-difficulty Sunken Throne uses 50K/100K/300K/500K Combat Power.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: weekly and beginner priorities change. Verify unlock, server sharing, ticket cost, completion credit, rewards and prerequisites before prescribing a route.

  • • Other change

    November 11 source corrections

    The revised KR source limits bound-Kina monster drops to Lower Reshanta, expands Advanced Breakthrough Material inputs to Expedition/Transcendence, makes territory editing PC-only, and deletes Liberated White Night Ring from the announced new dungeon/crafted equipment list.

    BeforeNew equipment list included Liberated White Night Ring; earlier Lower/Middle, Transcendence-only and unspecified territory-edit wording→AfterLiberated White Night Ring deleted; Lower only; Expedition/Transcendence; territory editing PC-only

    Combat impact P1/P2 analysis: do not route players toward the deleted ring entry; retain the corrected Abyss, morph and territory guidance. The current evergreen site does not claim this ring, so only the hash-bound patch pages and evidence records need correction. Verify the live dungeon/crafting catalog, acquisition source, recipe and conversion before adding any gear route.

CLASS CHANGES

Brawler · KR ↔ Adjustment

Adjustment 1 · Bug fix 1

  • ↔ Adjustment

    Fighting Spirit windows

    Ground Slam and Iron Mountain grant Fighting Spirit for 2s, increasing Ascending Strike and Explosive Fist PvE damage by 150% and PvP damage by 50%. Ascending Strike changes 30s→10s with −36% PvE base damage; All-out changes 90s→60s and Focused Block 20s→10s.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: short Fighting Spirit windows determine the new sequence. Brawler is KR-only in this catalog and lacks a compatible Global base snapshot, so no cross-region eDPS is computed.

  • ✓ Bug fix

    Gale Strike resistance reduction

    Fixes specialization-2 status-resistance reduction not being removable by a healing potion.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: this is a cleanse-behavior fix without a damage value; verify the debuff icon, potion removal and reapplication.

02Class balance · all nine KR classes

  • Official fact: all nine KR classes have announced damage, cooldown, specialization or condition changes; Brawler remains KR-only in this catalog. Exact published cooldown overrides are recorded in data/skill-snapshot.json with this source hash.

03Bosses, dungeon gates and weekly routes

  • Official facts: level-55 Urugugu and Eracus use Combat Power gates; old modes are removed or consolidated; solo scaling, shared pity rules, Odyle Energy costs and Awakening Battle attempts change.
  • P0/P1 public targets: /dungeons/, /endgame/, /routines/ and Korean counterparts; source targets progression.py, dungeonmodes.py, routines.py and encounters.py. 04Abyss schedules and combat rules

  • Official facts: Surface upper gates are removed while level 45/item level 1000 minima remain; equalization, consumable limits, AP reduction, weekly time and Artifact/Watcher schedules change. Season 5 resets between its two announced halves.
  • P0 public targets: /routines/ and Abyss-related guide entries; source targets routines.py and advancedguides.py. Verify server clocks, matching, boss spawns, equalized stats/skills, contribution percentile rewards and reset state.

05Map and beginner progression

  • Official facts: the cap is 55, Eltnen/Morheim part 2 opens, and Large Hidden Cubes consume 10 keys for 10× rewards. New gear, inheritance costs and growth-support systems are also announced.
  • P1 public targets: /maps/, /start/, /endgame/ and Korean counterparts; source targets data/maps.json, mapdata.py, onboarding.py and progression.py. Verify coordinates, spawns, acquisition/reset conditions, unlocks, costs, rewards and prerequisites in game.
