PATCH NOTES / KR

November 13 · Korea

KR adjusts Gladiator, Templar, Assassin, Brawler, Ranger, Sorcerer and Spiritmaster rotations with explicit cooldown, MP, duration, specialization and condition changes. It also fixes Laur reflection, Atiel party stagger, several boss attacks and progression blockers. These November 13 rules stay KR-only and do not establish a measured DPS ranking or final tier.

CLASS CHANGES

Gladiator ↔ Adjustment

Adjustment 1 · Bug fix 1

Class guide →
  • ↔ Adjustment

    Ruinous Blow cycle and MP flow

    Ruinous Blow changes 40→60s, specialization-3 charge time 20→30s, Frenzy 9→10s and MP cost 250→100. Aerial Snare changes from 150 MP cost to 150 MP recovery, while Mocking Blade changes from 200 MP cost to 100 MP recovery.

    BeforeRuinous Blow 40s / charge 20s / Frenzy 9s / MP 250; Aerial Snare MP −150; Mocking Blade MP −200→AfterRuinous Blow 60s / charge 30s / Frenzy 10s / MP 100; Aerial Snare MP +150; Mocking Blade MP +100

    Combat impact P1 analysis: burst frequency falls while resource stability improves. Reproduce charges, Frenzy stacks, animation time and a full KR rotation before estimating eDPS.

  • ✓ Bug fix

    Frenzy multi-target stacking

    Fixes Frenzy gaining two stacks at once when one skill hits multiple targets.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: removes unintended resource gain without a published total-DPS percentage.

CLASS CHANGES

Templar ↑ Buff

Buff 1

Class guide →
  • ↑ Buff

    Judgment chain reliability

    Shield Smite and Doom Shield activate Judgment for 5s instead of 3s. Judgment specialization-3/5 input time changes 1→1.5s; specialization 3 changes its sequence bonuses from 20/50/100/200% to 50/100/150/200%, and specialization 5 requires three uses instead of five. Shield of Protection specialization 3 now resets and activates Judgment for 5s after a block.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: the chain is easier to complete and early repeated hits are stronger. Verify reset order, threat and block-trigger behavior.

CLASS CHANGES

Assassin ↑ Buff

Buff 1

Class guide →
  • ↑ Buff

    Guaranteed insignia generation

    Savage Roar MP changes 120→50 and its PvE/PvP damage rises 40%/30%. The obsolete follow-up is removed; Savage Kick gains one guaranteed insignia, Insignia Explosion changes 5→3s, and Savage Fang grants five insignias instead of three.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: random setup is reduced and five-stack access is faster. Verify chain removal, PC-specific specialization behavior and a full burst loop.

Ranger ↔ Adjustment

Adjustment 1

Class guide →
  • ↔ Adjustment

    Rupture Arrow Resonance trigger

    Rupture Arrow now grants Resonance on use while in Precision instead of on hit. Specialization 5 replaces a 35% cooldown-reset chance with +2s Resonance duration.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: activation becomes reliable but the probabilistic reset is removed. Reproduce misses, range and Explosive Arrow follow-ups.

CLASS CHANGES

Sorcerer ↑ Buff

Buff 1 · Bug fix 1

Class guide →
  • ↑ Buff

    Bittercold Wind delivery and Element Enhancement

    Bittercold Wind keeps its hit count and damage while cast duration is halved; specializations 1/5 reduce cooldown by 20s on cast. Element Enhancement changes 60→45s.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: damage is delivered faster with more reliable cooldown reduction. Tick timing, movement and rotation uptime still need KR-client reproduction.

  • ✓ Bug fix

    Winter Shard multi-target stacking and Laur reflection

    Fixes Winter’s Shackles stacking Winter Shard multiple times from one multi-target hit, and stops Laur from reflecting Bittercold Wind, Fire Wall and Cold Storm damage.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: removes unintended stacking and restores intended placed-skill safety; no arbitrary damage multiplier is assigned.

Spiritmaster ↑ Buff

Buff 1 · Bug fix 1

Class guide →

    Spirit uptime and combat speed

    Dimensional Control becomes 30s, no longer requires a summoned spirit and grants the summoned spirit 100% combat speed for 7s. Normal spirit summons change 30→10s. Spirit Descent changes 6→10s; Ancient Spirit specialization 2 replaces 1.5× Descent for 6s with −30s cooldown.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: summon recovery and burst windows expand. Pet attack cadence, resummon loss and Descent refresh order must be measured before eDPS use.

  • ✓ Bug fix

    Ancient Spirit Descent refresh

    Fixes Ancient Spirit failing to refresh Spirit Descent.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: restores intended buff refresh without a published total-DPS value.

    Fixes Atiel Earth Collapse excessive stagger in parties of two or more, excessive damage from a Stof skill, and Ulmar follow-up explosion timing plus effect/hit-area mismatch.

Adjustment 1

  • ↔ Adjustment

    Fighting Spirit windows and clone duration

    Fighting Spirit moves from Iron Mountain to Spinning Kick, Berserk and Ground Slam change 2→3s, Ground Slam specialization 2 changes from 10% life steal to 30% skill speed, Gale Strike specialization 1 changes to 15% life steal, All-out no longer forcibly ends Berserk, and Clone lasts 10.5s at level 1 plus 0.5s per level.

PATCH HIGHLIGHTS

Other patch highlights

01Region, publication and effective date

  • Published November 13 at 00:00:00 UTC, source-updated at 00:01:00 UTC and effective with the November 13 KR maintenance. This editorial review was completed separately at 08:40:09 UTC and is bound to official source hash c64838c4…; collection time is not used as review time.
  • KR only. The official maintenance notice now says service reopened and lists time-limited compensation, but compensation is not a recurring route reward. No change is copied to North America.

02Class inputs and guide targets

  • Official facts: seven KR classes receive explicit cooldown, MP, duration, damage, specialization or condition changes. Published numeric inputs are stored in data/skill-snapshot.json under this hash. Existing Global data/skills.json and historical DPS/rankings remain unchanged.
  • P1 public targets are /classes/gladiator/, /classes/templar/, /classes/assassin/, /classes/ranger/, /classes/sorcerer/, /classes/spiritmaster/ and their Korean counterparts, plus the corresponding /skills/<skill>/ routes. Brawler has no compatible Global class/skill route and remains in the KR patch record. Verify tooltips, coefficients, cooldowns, level scaling, condition gates, charges, animation time and reproducible rotations before changing evergreen builds.

03Boss mechanics and dungeon fixes

  • Official fixes cover Laur reflection, Atiel party stagger, Stof damage, Ulmar explosion telegraph/hit area, Nunu progression and a camp disconnect blocker. Exact public targets after direct verification are /dungeons/ and /guides/dungeon-rewards/ plus Korean counterparts; source targets fieldnotes.py, progression.py and encounters.py. No dedicated Atiel, Stof or Ulmar detail route currently exists.
  • Direct verification: actual region/difficulty, pattern trigger and response, one-player versus two-or-more-player stagger, damage logs, effect/hitbox alignment, disconnect recovery and re-entry. These fixes do not supply a damage coefficient or justify a DPS percentage.

04Growth route and review limits

  • Awakening Battle now counts for the Membership Pass mission. P1 targets after reproduction are /routines/ and /endgame/ plus Korean counterparts; source targets routines.py and progression.py. Verify mission unlock, clear counter, reward, prerequisite and reset.
  • This is a highlights review of every announced class group and guide-impacting fixes. It does not claim in-game validation, a measured rotation result or a final class tier. Historical observations retain their original dates and conditions.
