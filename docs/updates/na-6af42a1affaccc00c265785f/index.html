PATCH NOTES / NA

November 11 · North America

NA doubles Ludra’s run limit and improves its reactor and reward group, adds Fire Temple and Ferocious Horn Hard modes, extends consumables, increases weekly progression rewards and fixes three dungeon issues. No class coefficient or cooldown changes are announced.

CLASS CHANGES

All classes ↔ Adjustment

Adjustment 1 · Bug fix 4 · Other change 3

  • ↔ Adjustment

    Abyssal Forge: Ludra · time, reactor and rewards

    Normal and Hard run limits rise to two hours, Central Abyss Fusion Reactor HP is reduced, and Reward Group 5 is expanded with three Wrathful materials.

    BeforeRun limit 1 hour; Reward Group 5 drop rate 30%→AfterRun limit 2 hours; Reward Group 5 drop rate 100%; Wrathful Mind/Will/Ego added

    Combat impact P1 analysis: the current NA guide’s one-hour limit was outdated and is updated. Lower reactor HP and a guaranteed reward-group roll do not establish boss mechanics, clear difficulty or a guaranteed quantity of every listed item.

  • • Other change

    Fire Temple and Ferocious Horn · Hard mode

    Hard mode is added with item level 2800, a four-minute Frenzy timer and three revives per player, plus new guaranteed, chance, material and achievement rewards.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: the November 5 removal of incorrectly displayed Hard choices is superseded by this announced mode. Existing /dungeons/fire-temple/ and /dungeons/fierce-horn/ lessons retain older KR evidence until NA patterns and conditions are reproduced.

  • • Other change

    Consumables · duration and persistence

    Potions, food and scrolls whose effects lasted five minutes now last ten minutes and persist through death and resurrection.

    Before5-minute effect→After10-minute effect; persists after death/resurrection

    Combat impact P1 analysis: supply timing and repeat-run preparation change. Verify which item grades are included, remaining-duration behavior, dispels and instance transitions before changing evergreen budgets.

  • • Other change

    Weekly progression and Abyss rewards

    Transcendence Season Missions add up to three Unique Sync Stones per week, Shugo Festival gains an Abyss Point weekly mission, Duty AP rises, Lower Abyss monster Kina becomes bound and Chaotic Upper Reshanta AP increases.

    BeforeDuty reward: 1,000 AP→AfterDuty reward: 2,500 AP; up to 3 Unique Sync Stones per week

    Combat impact P1 analysis: weekly priorities and non-PvP AP routes improve, but the notice does not publish the Upper Reshanta increase or Shugo mission amount. Verify mission unlocks, completion counters, reset and actual rewards.

  • ✓ Bug fix

    Seal Dungeon · underwater gauge

    Fixes the underwater gauge occasionally decreasing immediately after entering certain underwater Seal Dungeons.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: this restores traversal time rather than adding damage. Verify the affected dungeon, initial gauge value and re-entry behavior.

  • ✓ Bug fix

    Zikel's Sanctum · quest progression

    Fixes an occasional progression block in the Asmodian Quest Dungeon, Zikel's Sanctum.

    Combat impact P0 analysis: an intermittent progression blocker is removed. Verify the exact step, prerequisite, trigger and recovery on an affected character.

  • ✓ Bug fix

    Ludra's Trial · wipe reset

    Fixes Ludra's Trial failing to reset after a party wipe during a dungeon mechanic.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: the intended retry state is restored without a published damage value. Verify the mechanic trigger, wipe, reset timing and re-entry state.

  • ✓ Bug fix

    November 4 guaranteed-reward restoration

    Affected characters who missed guaranteed Expedition rewards after the November 4 requirement reduction receive the dungeon-specific bound Armor Selection Chest in their Cube during maintenance.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: this is targeted restoration, not a permanent extra reward. Verify eligibility, chest name, delivery and expiration; do not add it to recurring route yields.

PATCH HIGHLIGHTS

Other patch highlights

01Region, publication and effective date

  • Published November 11 at 08:40:57 UTC for the NA/Global service, revised at 08:43:32 UTC and announced for the November 11 maintenance. This editorial review is bound to official source hash 690c4181…; collection time is not used as the review time.
  • NA only: none of these Ludra, Hard-mode, consumable, Abyss or weekly-reward rules are copied to Korea. No class coefficient, cooldown, skill-level effect, measured DPS or final tier is announced.

02Ludra · current guide correction

  • Official facts: both modes change from 60 to 120 minutes; reactor HP is reduced without a published value; Reward Group 5 rises from 30% to 100% and adds Wrathful Mind, Will and Ego. The run-limit correction is applied to /guides/na-ludra/ and /guides/endgame-crafts/ plus Korean counterparts; source targets ludra.py and advancedguides.py.
  • Direct verification remains for actual boss difficulty, reactor phase trigger/response, attack patterns, Frenzy, resurrection/re-entry, reward-group quantities and each item’s binding. The official HP reduction is not converted into a player DPS multiplier.

03New Hard modes · evidence boundary

  • Official facts: Fire Temple and Ferocious Horn Hard require item level 2800, use a four-minute Frenzy timer and allow three revives per player. Rewards include a chance and guaranteed choice for Philosopher’s Stone: Revelation, dungeon-specific Ariel Fragments, Unique materials, artwork, more Kina and achievement skins.
  • P1 targets after direct verification: /dungeons/fire-temple/, /dungeons/fierce-horn/ and /guides/dungeon-rewards/ plus Korean counterparts; source targets fieldnotes.py, progression.py and encounters.py. Verify region/difficulty, prerequisites, pattern triggers/responses, Frenzy start, revive reset, reward quantities, morph limits and reset. Existing KR Conquest animations remain labelled and are not presented as NA Hard mechanics.

04Weekly route, Abyss and consumables

  • P1 targets after verification: /routines/, /endgame/, /guides/shugo-festival/ and /guides/pvp-context/ plus Korean counterparts; source targets routines.py, progression.py and advancedguides.py. Verify mission unlocks and counters, Wednesday reset, Sync Stone grade, AP amounts, Lower-Abyss currency label and the unpublished Upper-Abyss increase.
  • The 5→10 minute consumable change and death persistence can affect preparation guidance, but included item grades, remaining duration, dispels and zone changes need reproduction. No price, consumption-rate or DPS saving is inferred.

05Fixes, restoration and review limits

  • Official fixes cover an underwater-gauge loss, a Zikel’s Sanctum progression blocker and Ludra’s Trial wipe reset. Affected November 4 guaranteed-reward cases receive bound Armor Selection Chests. No arbitrary damage percentage is assigned.
  • This is a highlights review of guide-impacting content. It does not claim complete republication of paid skin tables, in-game validation or a final class tier. Historical observations and rankings retain their original dates and conditions.
