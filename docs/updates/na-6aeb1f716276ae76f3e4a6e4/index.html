PATCH NOTES / NA

November 4 hotfix · North America

NA removes Hard difficulty options that were unintentionally displayed for the Fire Temple and Ferocious Horn Den Expeditions. NC announces no new difficulty, boss-mechanic, reward or class-value change; restart after updating through Steam or PURPLE.

Bug fix 1 · Other change 1

  • ✓ Bug fix

    Fire Temple and Ferocious Horn Den · difficulty display

    Fixes Hard difficulty being unintentionally shown as an option for the Fire Temple and Ferocious Horn Den Expeditions.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: do not plan entry, rewards or progression around the removed option. This is an availability/UI correction, not evidence of changed Normal-mode mechanics or damage.

  • • Other change

    Client update required

    Exit AION 2, update through the Steam or PURPLE launcher, and restart the game to apply the hotfix.

    Combat impact P2 analysis: an already-running client can retain the stale option until it is updated and restarted.

Other patch highlights

01Region, dates and confirmed fix

  • Published November 4 at 12:00 UTC and revised at 12:38:32 UTC for North America. The hotfix is bound to official source hash 44e5f9c2… and requires a Steam or PURPLE launcher update plus a game restart.
  • NA-only: this does not change Korea. NC states only that the Hard options were unintended; it publishes no entry item level, reward, boss trigger, counter, enrage, revive, coefficient, cooldown or skill-level value.

02Guide impact and direct verification

  • P1 public targets are /updates/na-6aeb1f716276ae76f3e4a6e4/ and /ko/updates/na-6aeb1f716276ae76f3e4a6e4/ from data/patch-reviews.json. Existing /dungeons/fire-temple/ and /dungeons/fierce-horn/ pages remain explicitly scoped to older KR Conquest evidence and are not rewritten as NA Hard guides.
