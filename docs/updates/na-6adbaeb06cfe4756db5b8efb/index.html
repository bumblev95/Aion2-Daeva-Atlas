PATCH NOTES / NA

November 4 · North America Ludra preview

NA preview: Ludra arrives November 4 with new mechanics. Normal requires item level 2800 and Hard requires 3000. NC also announced a Hard-clear race, daily Odyle Energy support and a dungeon-clear community event; detailed attack patterns and reward/drop rates were not published.

    Ludra · Normal and Hard launch

    Ludra becomes available in North America on November 4 with Normal and Hard difficulties and newly announced mechanics.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: this creates a new endgame preparation target, but NC has not published attack triggers, counters, enrage timing, revive rules or drop rates. Do not publish a pattern guide from the preview alone.

  • • Other change

    Ludra Hunt · clear recognition

    From November 4 maintenance until November 24 maintenance (PST), the first 20 Hard-clear parties in each listed region receive ranked titles; other Normal or Hard clears receive the Ludra Hunter title.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: clear timing and party eligibility matter for the event, but ranking is regional and is not evidence of class strength or measured DPS.

  • • Other change

    Odyle Energy Boost

    From October 28 after maintenance through November 10 before maintenance (PST), each character can claim two Odyle Energy from in-game mail once per day; each mail expires after seven days.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: this temporarily increases available Expedition and Transcendence Dungeon runs. Actual mailbox reset, claim prerequisites and regional maintenance timing require client verification.

PATCH HIGHLIGHTS

Other patch highlights

01Region, announcement and effective dates

  • Published October 23 at 19:00 UTC and revised at 19:00:27 UTC for the North American service. Ludra and its related events are scheduled for November 4; the Odyle Energy support starts after the October 28 maintenance.
  • NA-only: do not apply this schedule, item-level gate or event eligibility to Korea. No class skill, coefficient, cooldown or level-effect changes are announced.

02Ludra encounter facts and guide impact

  • Official fact: Normal and Hard open immediately at item levels 2800 and 3000. The notice says there are new mechanics but does not identify the region/arena, pattern conditions, counters, animations, enrage timer, revive limit or loot probabilities.
  • P1 guide impact: publish the source-bound preview at /updates/na-6adbaeb06cfe4756db5b8efb/ and /ko/updates/na-6adbaeb06cfe4756db5b8efb/. Current main has no dedicated Ludra dungeon page, so no route or boss-pattern animation is invented from this announcement.
  • Direct verification pending: difficulty labels and item-level gate, party size and eligibility, every attack trigger/counter, enrage and revive rules, clear recognition, title delivery and rewards in the NA client after November 4.

03Temporary growth support and priorities

  • The two daily Odyle Energy items are sent per character and expire from mail after seven days. NC says they can be used for more Expeditions and Transcendence Dungeons; it does not announce a permanent reset or cap change.
  • Daeva Bootcamp counts eligible regional clears of Krao Cave, Draupnir, Urugugu Canyon and Vakron Sky Island (Normal/Hard), plus every stage of Shattered Arkanis and Deus Research Base. Rewards unlock at global milestones; this review does not recreate the full reward or Twitch Drop tables.
  • P1 guide targets after verification: /start/, /endgame/ and /guides/dungeon-rewards/ in both languages. Confirm mailbox reset, character/account scope, event unlocks, eligible-dungeon recognition, reward delivery and prerequisites before changing evergreen priorities.

04Review scope and preserved research

  • This is a highlights review of the boss launch and progression-relevant events, not a complete republication of login rewards, Twitch Drops, cosmetics, contest rules or every reward row. No in-game validation is claimed.
  • Exact source-bound data paths are data/patch-reviews.json and data/news.json. data/skills.json, data/skill-snapshot.json, data/coverage-audit.json inputs and historical DPS/ranking observations remain unchanged because no numeric skill change or measured combat result is supplied.
