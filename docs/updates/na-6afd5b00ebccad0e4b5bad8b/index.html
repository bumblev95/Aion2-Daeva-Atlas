PATCH NOTES / NA

November 18 · North America

NA adds Ludra Easy at item level 2,800 with unlimited personal revives, raises the combined Ludra weapon/guard rate to 15% on all three difficulties, halves the weapon-chest morph fee to 5,000,000 Kina, fixes unspecified Ludra patterns and makes field-monster Kina bound. No class coefficient, cooldown or skill-level effect is announced.

CLASS CHANGES

Buff 1 · Bug fix 2 · Other change 4

  • • Other change

    Ludra Easy entry and revival rules

    Abyssal Forge: Ludra Easy is added with a two-hour limit, minimum item level 2,800 and unlimited personal revives. It has Normal-mode HP, easier mechanics and stats adjusted so critical hits land more easily.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: Easy becomes the lowest-risk NA learning route, but the notice does not publish attack-by-attack counters. Verify unlocks, entry consumption, the exact mechanics retained or removed, critical-hit behavior, wipe/re-entry and reward cadence.

  • ↑ Buff

    Ludra gear combined drop rate

    The combined Ludra gear rate on Normal and Hard increases from 5% to 15%; Easy also uses a 15% combined rate. The Easy reward list names Ludra Weapon and Guard (Bound).

    BeforeNormal/Hard combined rate: 5%→AfterEasy/Normal/Hard combined rate: 15%

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: weapon/guard farming improves, but 15% is a combined category rate and does not promise either item per clear. Verify category contents, per-item distribution, reward eligibility and weekly limits.

  • • Other change

    Ludra weapon-chest morph costs

    The weapon-selection chest fee changes from 10,000,000 to 5,000,000 Kina. One Hard, two Normal or four Easy Gear Change Vouchers produce one chest; prior Hard-voucher users receive one Hard voucher retroactively.

    Before10,000,000 Kina→After5,000,000 Kina; Hard ×1 / Normal ×2 / Easy ×4

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: the upgrade path costs less Kina and now supports Easy vouchers. Verify the live morph preview, binding, chest contents, eligibility and retroactive Cube delivery before consuming vouchers.

  • • Other change

    Abyss Points boost event

    From November 17 maintenance through December 16 maintenance PST, specified Outer Abyss monster drops and Battlefield victory participation/mission rewards grant triple Abyss Points. Acquisition caps and Outer Abyss PvP AP are unchanged.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: NA routine priorities temporarily change for the listed sources only. Verify server-local start/end, eligible reward rows and cap accounting; do not triple PvP AP or the cap.

  • ✓ Bug fix

    Ludra attack patterns

    Fixes some Ludra attack patterns not functioning as intended.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: older NA recordings may show invalid behavior. The source names no pattern, trigger or counter, so reproduce each difficulty and record telegraph, boss HP, target state and outcome before changing a boss guide or animation.

  • • Other change

    Field-monster Kina becomes bound

    Kina from all field monsters changes to Bound Kina, and the daily 1,000,000 Kina acquisition limit is likewise tracked as Bound Kina.

    BeforeField-monster Kina; daily limit 1,000,000 Kina→AfterField-monster Bound Kina; daily limit 1,000,000 Bound Kina

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: field farming no longer supplies freely transferable Kina through this reward. Verify which costs accept Bound Kina, account/character scope, HUD counter and reset before rewriting beginner or farming budgets.

  • ✓ Bug fix

    Auto-translation and chat scroll

    Fixes untranslated text with Auto-Translation and chat jumping to the newest message while reading history.

    Combat impact P2 interpretation: communication and chat review should be more reliable. Verify language pairs, channels, history position and mobile/desktop behavior.

PATCH HIGHLIGHTS

Other patch highlights

01Region, publication and effective date

  • Published November 18 at 08:00:00 UTC and source-updated at 08:00:46 UTC for the North American service. The gameplay changes apply after the November 17 scheduled maintenance PST / November 18 CET; the AP event ends before December 16 maintenance PST / December 17 CET.
  • NA only, shared by the United States and Canada. None of these Ludra, AP or Bound-Kina rules is copied to Korea. No class coefficient, cooldown, skill-level effect, measured DPS or final tier is announced.

02Ludra Easy, rewards and morphs

  • Official facts: Easy uses item level 2,800, two hours and unlimited personal revives; it has Normal HP with easier mechanics and easier critical-hit conditions. Easy/Normal/Hard share a 15% combined Ludra gear rate, while the weapon-chest recipe costs 5,000,000 Kina and uses Easy ×4, Normal ×2 or Hard ×1 voucher.
  • P1 exact public targets updated from the official values are /guides/na-ludra/, /guides/endgame-crafts/, /guides/dungeon-rewards/ and their Korean counterparts; source targets are ludra.py, advancedguides.py and progression.py. Direct checks remain for unlocks, entry/reward consumption, retained/removed mechanics, critical-hit behavior, wipe/re-entry, combined-rate composition, morph preview and retroactive delivery.

03Pattern fix, AP event and Bound Kina

  • Official facts: some unnamed Ludra patterns are fixed; selected Outer Abyss monster and Battlefield victory rewards grant triple AP without changing caps; all field-monster Kina and its 1,000,000 daily limit become Bound Kina. The patch also fixes Auto-Translation and chat-history scrolling.
  • P1 targets after direct verification are /guides/na-ludra/, /routines/, /guides/kinah-farming/, /start/ and /endgame/ plus Korean counterparts; source targets include ludra.py, routines.py, progression.py and fieldnotes.py. No boss animation is changed until the affected pattern, difficulty, trigger and response are reproduced.

04Highlights review limits

  • This highlights review covers the guide-impacting systems, event rule and fixes. It does not claim live gameplay validation, complete event presentation, patch-derived DPS rankings or final class tiers. data/skills.json, data/skill-snapshot.json and historical DPS observations remain unchanged.
