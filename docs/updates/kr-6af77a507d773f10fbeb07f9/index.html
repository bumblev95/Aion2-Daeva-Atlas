PATCH NOTES / KR

November 14 · Korea

KR adjusts all nine classes with cooldown, PvE/PvP damage, resource, specialization and condition changes; fixes a Spiritmaster combat-speed application issue; lowers two Sanctuary bosses’ HP by 10%; and increases Arena/Abyss healing reduction from 20% to 50%. These November 14 rules stay KR-only and do not establish measured DPS rankings or final tiers.

CLASS CHANGES

Assassin ↔ Adjustment

Adjustment 2 · Bug fix 1

Class guide →
  • ↔ Adjustment

    Killing Intent and Heart Gore loop

    Ambush changes 10→15s, grants 3s of Killing Intent with +5% Crushing Blow, and deals 10 instead of 7 stagger. Heart Gore gains three consecutive uses and its Killing Intent bonus changes 70%→150%; specialization-2 rear damage changes 20%→30%.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: the burst sequence moves toward Ambush→three Heart Gore uses. Verify rear-position checks, Killing Intent consumption, animation time and a repeatable full loop before eDPS use.

  • ↔ Adjustment

    Insignia setup and Savage Fang

    Insignia Explosion changes 3→7s, removes Killing Intent, resets Heart Gore after five insignias and leaves two insignias with specialization 3. Savage Fang gains 20% PvE damage and grants Killing Intent for 5s with specialization 1; Exploit Weakness clones now add an insignia.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: five-stack resets and deterministic insignia generation can offset the longer Explosion cooldown. Verify stack timing, reset order, specialization 5’s −2s Ambush effect and the complete burst cycle.

  • ✓ Bug fix

    Savage chain use frequency

    Fixes Savage Roar and Savage Kick reducing the use frequency of other skills.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: restores intended rotation selection without a published damage value. No arbitrary DPS percentage is assigned; reproduce auto-use priority and manual chains.

CLASS CHANGES

Ranger ↔ Adjustment

Nerf 1 · Adjustment 1

Class guide →

    Shackle conditions and resource flow

    Snare Shot applies 3s Shackle with −30% status resistance instead of Slow; Rupture Arrow and Rooting Eye use Shackle conditions. Tempest Shot MP changes 120→70, Marking Shot loses +20% status accuracy in Precision, and Explosion Trap specialization 3 replaces delayed damage with shield removal.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: control chains become more consistent while one accuracy bonus and delayed-damage option disappear. Verify Shackle icon, resistance effect, condition gates, MP and shield removal.

  • ↓ Nerf

    Arrow Scattershot PvP and life steal

    Arrow Scattershot PvP damage falls 30%, and specialization-1 life steal changes 5%→1.5%.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: this reduces KR PvP pressure and sustain. It is not applied to NA PvE DPS; verify the PvP tooltip and healing result.

Sorcerer ↔ Adjustment

Adjustment 2

    Fire Energy and cooldown routing

    Hellfire grants Fire Energy for 5s and deals +150% damage on hit while active. Wish of Concentration specialization 5 becomes −10s to all skill cooldowns. Bittercold Wind specializations are rearranged, including up to +20% damage with fewer targets and −20s Freezing cooldown.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: cast order and single-target burst change materially. Verify Fire Energy consumption, hit timing, target-count scaling and exact cooldown application before eDPS use.

  • ↔ Adjustment

    Winter and elemental cooldowns

    Winter’s Shackles changes 40→45s and its specializations are reworked. Element Enhancement, Fire Wall and Cold Storm each change 45→60s.

    Combat impact P1 analysis: several setup skills become less frequent while new specialization reductions may offset parts of the loss. Reproduce summon ticks, stacks and a full rotation; do not revise historical DPS.

PATCH HIGHLIGHTS

Other patch highlights

01Region, publication and effective date

  • Published November 13 at 21:00:00 UTC, source-updated at 21:01:00 UTC and effective with the November 14 KR maintenance. This editorial review was completed separately at 08:38:50 UTC and is bound to official source hash ff916770…; collection time is not used as review time.
  • KR only. No class, PvP or Sanctuary rule is copied to the North America feed. Historical combat observations keep their original dates, builds and conditions.

02Class inputs and exact public targets

  • Official facts: all nine KR classes receive a cooldown, damage, resource, specialization, condition or bug-fix change. Explicit numeric inputs are stored in data/skill-snapshot.json under this hash. Global data/skills.json remains the base snapshot and is not overwritten.
  • P1 public targets are /classes/gladiator/, /classes/templar/, /classes/assassin/, /classes/chanter/, /classes/ranger/, /classes/sorcerer/, /classes/cleric/, /classes/spiritmaster/ and Korean counterparts, plus the matching /skills/<skill>/ routes. Brawler remains KR-only without a compatible public class route. Verify tooltips, coefficients, cooldowns, skill-level scaling, condition gates, animation time and reproducible rotations.

03PvP and Sanctuary impact

  • Official facts: KR Arena/Abyss healing reduction is 50%, and Muspel’s Chalice/Snowfield of Grief boss HP is 10% lower. P1 public targets after direct verification are /guides/pvp-context/, /dungeons/, /guides/dungeon-rewards/ and /guides/kr-bitan/ with Korean counterparts; source targets advancedguides.py, progression.py and encounters.py.
  • Direct verification: actual mode/difficulty/region, healing and shield interactions, displayed boss HP, phase triggers/responses, enrage and reward eligibility. The notice does not publish new coordinates, spawn rules, rewards, resets or beginner unlock/cost changes, so map and growth data stay unchanged.

04Review limits

  • This highlights review covers every announced class group and the two guide-impacting systems. It does not claim in-game validation, complete event/product-table republication, measured rotation results, patch-derived DPS rankings or final class tiers.
