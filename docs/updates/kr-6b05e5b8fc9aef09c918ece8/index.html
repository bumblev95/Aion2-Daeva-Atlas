PATCH NOTES / KR

November 25 · Korea

KR adds solo entry to selected Expedition, Conquest and Transcendence modes with a front/rear damage-conversion buff, rematches the Abyss, introduces negative Kina restrictions and expands base Cube slots from 40 to 50. Assassin Killing Intent changes from 5s to 7s while its Crushing Blow increase changes from 3% to 2%; several classes receive input-cancel, tooltip, stacking, NPC-effect and specialization fixes. These values remain KR-only and do not recalculate NA PvE, historical DPS or final tiers.

CLASS CHANGES

All classes ↑ Buff

Buff 1 · Bug fix 1 · Other change 4

  • • Other change

    Solo-mode entry and positional-damage conversion

    Selected Expedition, Conquest and Transcendence modes gain a solo entry button. In solo mode, 50% of the character’s Rear Damage Amplification stat is added to Front Damage Amplification without reducing the rear value.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: solo positioning becomes less punitive, but this is a conversion of the stat value rather than a flat 50% damage bonus. Verify each listed mode, party lock, retry behavior, displayed stats, rounding and front/rear hit tests before changing route or eDPS assumptions.

  • • Other change

    Abyss rematch and reset

    KR Abyss servers are rematched; Artifact occupation states and Abyss Corridor progress reset with the new schedule.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: a fixed opponent or schedule can be stale. Verify the selected server group, activation time, Artifact state, Corridor reset and later rotations before updating a persistent PvP route.

  • • Other change

    Trust limits and negative Kina

    Marketplace settlement limits based on Trust resume after KR server transfer ends. Accounts flagged for abnormal transactions can receive a negative Kina balance that restricts Market, Exchange and server-transfer functions until unbound Kina earnings restore the balance to zero or above.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: affected accounts have a different trading and transfer path. Verify the displayed balance, restricted actions, qualifying unbound-Kina sources, debit order and recovery threshold; do not copy this KR rule into the NA Kinah guide.

  • ↑ Buff

    Base Cube slots

    Base Cube capacity increases by 10 slots, including characters that already expanded their Cube.

    Before40 base slots→After50 base slots

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: storage pressure falls, but verify total capacity, existing expansions, account/character scope and post-maintenance delivery before revising inventory advice.

  • ✓ Bug fix

    Laur and Atiel encounter behavior

    Fixes Laur’s Final Execution effect activating after Laur dies in Fanatic Urugugu Canyon, and Atiel’s Stagger Gauge failing to recover normally after Earth Collapse in Infested Deus Research Base.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: older recordings may show invalid behavior. Reproduce difficulty, phase, trigger, telegraph, boss state, gauge recovery and response before changing boss text or animation.

  • • Other change

    Poeta and Ishalgen warehouse managers

    Warehouse managers are added to Poeta and Ishalgen.

    Combat impact P2 interpretation: starter-zone inventory routing can change. Verify the in-game coordinates, map marker, interaction requirement and storage scope before adding map pins.

PATCH HIGHLIGHTS

Other patch highlights

01Region, publication and effective date

  • Published November 24 at 19:30:00 UTC and source-updated at 19:30:54 UTC for the Korean service. The changes take effect after the November 25 KR scheduled maintenance; the editorial review time above is separate from collection and publication timestamps.
  • KR only. No class, solo-mode, Abyss, negative-Kina, Cube or map rule here is applied to the United States/Canada feed. Historical combat records retain their original period and conditions.

02Class changes and research inputs

  • Official fact: Assassin Killing Intent changes 5→7s and Crushing Blow +3%→+2% for Ambush and Savage Fang. Those explicit values are recorded as a new KR-only override in data/skill-snapshot.json. data/skills.json, the incompatible Global base, NA PvE and historical rankings remain unchanged.
  • P1 exact public targets are /classes/assassin/, /skills/ambush/, /skills/savage-fang/ and the Korean counterparts; other class facts remain on /updates/kr-6b05e5b8fc9aef09c918ece8/ until client verification. Source targets are data/skills.json and data/skill-snapshot.json. Verify tooltips, duration, Crushing Blow stacking, input cancellation, conditions, animation time and reproducible rotations. Storm Order’s corrected value and Explosive Arrow’s cooldown amount are not published, so no numeric input is added.

03Solo modes, bosses and Abyss

  • Official solo-mode list: Draupnir, Urugugu Canyon, Fire Temple, Cradle of Nothingness, Blue Breath Island, Fallen Daeva Castle Normal/Hard, Infested Deus Research Base Normal/Hard, Fanatic Urugugu Canyon Normal/Hard, and Sunken Temple of Life stages 1–4. Solo entry blocks party invitation/recruitment and retry remains solo.
  • P1 exact public targets after client verification are /dungeons/, /dungeons/urugugu-canyon/, /guides/dungeon-rewards/, /guides/abyss-outland/ and /routines/ plus Korean counterparts; source targets are progression.py, fieldnotes.py, encounters.py, outland.py and routines.py. Verify each mode’s difficulty and region, unlock, solo scaling, party restrictions, reward consumption and reset; reproduce Laur and Atiel triggers and responses before changing boss animations.
  • The published 50% rule converts half of the Rear Damage Amplification stat into additional Front Damage Amplification in solo mode; it does not halve rear damage and is not a universal 50% damage buff. No measured eDPS or final tier is derived from it.

04Growth, map and economy

  • P1/P2 targets after direct verification are /guides/kinah-farming/, /maps/, /start/ and /endgame/ plus Korean counterparts; source targets are progression.py, data/maps.json, worldmap.py and onboarding.py. Verify negative-balance restrictions and recovery, Cube capacity and existing expansions, and the warehouse manager coordinates, marker, prerequisites and storage scope.
  • No map coordinate is invented, and the KR negative-Kina rule does not rewrite the NA World Market guidance. The promotion and product tables remain in the official source and are outside this evergreen highlights review.

05Highlights review limits

  • This highlights review covers every class section and the guide-impacting combat, dungeon, boss, map, growth and economy changes. It does not claim complete republication of Black Friday event/product tables, live gameplay validation, patch-derived DPS rankings or final class tiers.
