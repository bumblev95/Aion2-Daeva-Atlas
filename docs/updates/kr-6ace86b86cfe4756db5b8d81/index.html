CLASS CHANGES

Gladiator ↔ Adjustment

Buff 2 · Nerf 1

Class guide →
  • ↑ Buff

    Assault · defensive effect

    The five-second, hit-count-dependent defense bonus rises from a 20% maximum to an 80% maximum and now includes all elemental resistance.

    BeforeUp to +20% defense for 5s→AfterUp to +80% defense and all elemental resistance for 5s

    Combat impact Can improve survival after hitting multiple enemies; the maximum benefit still depends on the number hit.

  • ↓ Nerf

    Assault · cooldown interaction

    Cooldown-reduction effects no longer apply to this Assault skill.

    Combat impact The stronger defense comes with reduced access to cooldown-driven re-engagement; the combined change is an adjustment.

  • ↑ Buff

    Defensive skill · cooldown

    Successful blocking now reduces this defensive skill’s cooldown by 0.3 seconds.

    Combat impact Successful defense brings the next defensive use closer; this improves survival rather than the attack damage coefficient.

    Combat impact This text correction alone does not establish a damage increase.

    Stops specialization-3 bonus damage from occurring after Embers has already ended.

    Combat impact Removing unintended post-DoT hits can reduce damage in that situation; a bug fix can have a negative damage effect.

    Cold Storm · Frostbite uptime

    A higher-level Cold Storm from another player no longer removes your Frostbite.

    Combat impact Can preserve your Frostbite damage in multi-Sorcerer encounters.

  • ↔ Adjustment

    Attack-skill elemental assignment

    Elemental damage is assigned to these skills (KR names retained to avoid guessing regional skill names): Fire: 루미엘의 공간, 나락, 강화의 은혜, 생기 증발. Water: 영혼 동결, 냉기 소환.

    Combat impact Actual damage depends on elemental damage/resistance conditions; this is not a universal buff or a fixed DPS multiplier.

PATCH HIGHLIGHTS

Other patch highlights

01Inheritance materials and Legion UI

  • Replaces slot-specific inheritance stones with Unique/Heroic inheritance flames. Old stones become faded items; both old stones and fragments can be morphed into the new materials.
  • Legion lists show the dungeon members are in and support item-level/Combat-Power sorting.

02Snowfield first-clear rewards

  • Mails the title and gold Void pet to the first clearing force, silver Void to clears 2–5, and bronze Void to clears 6–20. Mail can be claimed for one month.

03Halloween attendance, missions and shop

  • Runs from maintenance October 14 until maintenance November 4. Attendance resets at 00:00, with 35 coins on ordinary days and 100 each on days 7 and 14.
  • KR event missions reset weekly per character. Abyss, Transcendence, Expedition, Ascension and Nightmare give 25 coins each, three logins 30, and gear, scroll, invasion and Shugo 20 each.
  • Town shops offer the chest skin for 15 coins with a server cap of one, and Odyle Energy for 10 coins with a server cap of four and a level-50 requirement. Challenge tickets, growth materials and combat consumables have separate official limits.

04Expedition and Transcendence Halloween boost

  • Specified Expedition Normal/Hard and Transcendence tiers 1–4 gain 1.5× enhancement-stone Odyle Cube rewards. 붉은 연심의 거울 separately gains enhancement stones; the source table lists eligible dungeons.
  • Eligible dungeon/difficulty combinations give three or five Candy Key Fragments. Colored keys cost 100 fragments, limited to one per color per server weekly; NA uses 60 fragments instead.
  • Red candy includes 10,000 Soul Crystals, yellow 30,000 enhancement stones and blue four Odyle Energy; each also includes additional growth materials and consumables.

05Boss, defense and settings fixes

  • Repairs defense stats failing to rise above a certain amount and resurrection-count reset failures on Trial/Sanctuary retries.
  • Slightly expands Kaira/Agro collision areas, clears lingering effects after Atiel dies, and halves Deltras’s Ice Wedge HP.
  • Adds camera Y-axis inversion and per-component own-nameplate visibility. Manually clicked drops bypass loot filters; the general-consumables filter affects only ordinary-or-lower-grade items.
  • Repairs inheritance-flame morphing for the named bound gear and missing racial-understanding achievements after Express-ticket use.

06Restoration and cosmetic products

  • Uses pre-October-7 Hidden Cube logs to restore the Soul Crystal reward difference and convert it to pet racial-understanding points. Auto-loot-ticket Kina restoration follows purchase/sale/recovery history.
  • Transferred characters not yet logged in receive restoration later. Adds armor/weapon/pet/wing/helm/decor cosmetics; the full product tables remain in the official notice.
