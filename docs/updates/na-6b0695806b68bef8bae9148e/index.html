PATCH NOTES / NA

November 25 · North America

NA changes Templar PvP, buffs Ranger cooldown and specialization values, removes two Chanter block/evasion bypasses and restructures two Spiritmaster effects. It also expands Stigma access, weekly Sync Stones, Abyss comeback rules, World trading and negative-Kina restrictions, and adds two instanced activities. These rules apply to the United States and Canada only; they do not rewrite KR, historical DPS or final tiers.

CLASS CHANGES

Spiritmaster ↔ Adjustment

Adjustment 2

Class guide →
  • ↔ Adjustment

    Earth Tremor Attack stack model

    Cold Shock/Vacuum Explosion/Earth Tremor Specialty 4 changes Earth Tremor from 2% Attack per stack, maximum five, to 10% Attack with maximum one; the granted effect can no longer be removed by buff-removal skills.

    BeforeAttack +2% per stack, max 5→AfterAttack +10%, max 1; cannot be removed by buff removal

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: the maximum remains 10%, while ramp and removal behavior change. Verify grant timing, duration, refresh, dispel immunity and rotation uptime; do not label this as a flat 5× damage buff.

  • ↔ Adjustment

    Element Unification level scaling

    Element Unification Critical Hit Damage changes from 1.1% to 5.5% at level 1 and from +0.1% to +0.5% per level, while the effect becomes non-stackable.

    BeforeLevel 1: 1.1%; +0.1% per level; stackable→AfterLevel 1: 5.5%; +0.5% per level; non-stackable

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: one caster supplies a stronger level-scaled effect while duplicate stacking is removed. Verify level breakpoints, party ownership, overwrite/refresh and summon inheritance before modeling party eDPS.

CLASS CHANGES

All classes ✓ Bug fix

Bug fix 1 · Other change 7

  • • Other change

    Crobakhi daily dungeon

    Crobakhi’s Secret Depository is added as a score-based Bound-Kina daily dungeon and shares its entry count with Daeva Bio-Research Base and Odylium Repository.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: choosing it consumes the same daily pool. Verify unlock, score thresholds, reward amounts, entry/reward reset and binding in the NA client before rewriting a farming route.

  • • Other change

    Stigma slots and shard supply

    Up to five Stigma skills can be equipped. The patch says a total of 50 new Bound Shards will be added through expanded and future sources; current named changes raise Nightmare and Shugo Festival character limits from 35 to 50 and add 20 at Wind Breeze Merchants for 250,000 Kina each.

    BeforeNightmare/Shugo limits: 35 per character→AfterUp to 5 equipped; Nightmare/Shugo 50; Wind Breeze 20 × 250,000 Kina

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: NA build and growth priorities gain one equipped slot and more named supply. Verify unlock prerequisites, full-versus-fragment item, currency, reset and character/server scope; this does not modify the dated KR shop table.

  • • Other change

    Weekly Sync Stones

    The Transcendence season-mission weekly maximum rises from three to twelve; Way of the Transcended steps 1–3 each award four Unique Sync Stones (Bound).

    Before3 per week→After12 per week; 4 at each of steps 1–3

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: weekly gear-option work accelerates. Verify mission unlocks, completion conditions, character/account scope, binding and Wednesday reset before changing routine totals.

  • • Other change

    Abyss Corridor comeback rule

    When the opposing faction captures all artifacts during Artifact Siege, a corridor appears at the disadvantaged faction base; Abyss Points gained in it are reduced by 30%.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: the corridor is an access comeback with an AP penalty, not a full-value farming route. Verify siege state, spawn/despawn, destination, eligible AP sources and rounding.

  • • Other change

    World Market and World Exchange

    World Market and World Exchange are added with a 20% transaction tax.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: NA gains a separate cross-world venue with a large sale deduction. Verify eligibility, listing/settlement limits, server-versus-world inventory and exact tax display before budget changes.

  • • Other change

    Negative Kina restrictions

    Negative Kina is introduced. A negative balance blocks Market buying/listing/settlement, Exchange listing/settlement and server transfer; specified unbound-Kina sources recover the balance and restrictions lift at zero or above.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: debt can stop core trading and transfer actions. Verify which balances and sources count, partial recovery, UI warnings and all blocked actions; do not infer unspecified spending restrictions.

  • • Other change

    Corroded Decontamination Facility

    The limited Sanctuary Corroded Decontamination Facility uses normalized stats, item level 2,100, seven weekly entries and Bargott as boss; the reward cube gives 5,000 event coins removed each Wednesday.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: it is a weekly event route, but the source does not publish Bargott patterns or the Odyle Energy cube cost. Verify unlock, region, normalization, entry/reward reset, energy cost, coin removal and every trigger/counter before adding boss guidance.

  • ✓ Bug fix

    RvR opposing-faction targeting

    Fixes an issue where the opposing faction could not be targeted during RvR.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: targeting should be restored, but no damage increase is announced. Reproduce tab/click/assist targeting by mode and faction before changing combat advice.

PATCH HIGHLIGHTS

Other patch highlights

01Region, publication and effective date

  • Published November 25 at 08:00:00 UTC and source-updated at 08:07:02 UTC. The gameplay rules apply to the North American service after the November 24 scheduled maintenance PST / November 25 CET.
  • NA only, shared by the United States and Canada. No value is copied to Korea. This review is bound to source hash bba5bd32a7e29dcee97c56240978d8db47f1e0c091cefda2f39d652638b279fc.

02Classes and research inputs

  • Official facts: Templar receives approximate PvP reductions; Ranger Marking Shot changes 5→10% and Bow of Blessing 120→90s; Chanter loses two Block/Evasion bypass clauses; Spiritmaster changes the Earth Tremor stack model and Element Unification level scaling/stackability.
  • P1 targets are /updates/na-6b0695806b68bef8bae9148e/, /classes/templar/, /classes/ranger/, /classes/chanter/, /classes/spiritmaster/ and the named /skills/ pages plus Korean-language counterparts. Source targets are data/patch-reviews.json and data/skill-snapshot.json. Direct checks remain for tooltip values, conditions, animation, hit results, stacking/removal and reproducible rotations. Approximate PvP values are not converted into exact coefficients, PvE DPS, historical records or final tiers.

03Stigma, growth and daily/weekly routes

  • Official facts: NA allows up to five equipped Stigma skills; Nightmare and Shugo Festival limits change 35→50, Wind Breeze Merchants sell 20 at 250,000 Kina each, weekly Sync Stones change 3→12, and Crobakhi shares a daily-entry pool with two existing dungeons.
  • P1 public targets are /guides/stigma-growth/, /skills/, /routines/, /endgame/ and /guides/kinah-farming/ plus Korean counterparts; source targets are advancedguides.py, skillbook.py, routines.py, progression.py and onboarding.py. The dated KR shop table remains separate. Verify prerequisites, costs, item types, scope, shared entries, rewards and resets in game.

04Abyss and economy systems

  • Official facts: the disadvantaged-faction Abyss Corridor carries a 30% AP reduction, World Market/Exchange use a 20% transaction tax, and a negative Kina balance blocks the listed trading, settlement and server-transfer actions until recovery.
  • P1 targets are /guides/abyss-outland/, /guides/kinah-farming/, /guides/server-transfer/, /start/, /endgame/ and /routines/ plus Korean counterparts; source targets include outland.py, progression.py, fieldnotes.py, onboarding.py and routines.py. Verify corridor triggers/destination/AP accounting, world-versus-server inventory, tax preview, debt sources and unblock timing.

05Event, fixes and review limits

  • The event facility’s item level, weekly entries, reward coins and reset are published, but Bargott patterns, triggers/counters and the reward-cube Odyle Energy cost are not. RvR targeting is a qualitative fix with no damage percentage.
  • This highlights review does not claim a complete reorganization of event/product tables or live gameplay validation. Exact boss guidance, map coordinates/spawns, unlock costs/rewards and rotations remain direct-verification work.
