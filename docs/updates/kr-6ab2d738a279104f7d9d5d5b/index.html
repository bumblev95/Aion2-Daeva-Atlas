02Abyss

  • Rematching resets ownership. The battlefield buff applies while eligible bosses exist in lower/middle zones and permanently inside the rift.
  • Eligible bosses are Agro, Kaira, the core, both Nahmas, the three executors, Dramos, Ducal and Maraka.
  • The buff replaces seven listed class buffs rather than stacking with them.

03Soul tuning

  • Tuning is faster and can repeat automatically with registered materials until a target option appears or materials run out.

04Shop

  • Atelier items gain hover previews; the grid uses four columns with previews, five without, and shades unavailable/sold-out offers.

05Wardrobe

  • Supported weapon skins gain paid customization presets previewable in the shop and wardrobe.

06Customization

  • Each gender gains three presets, nine hairstyles, two faces and one brow, plus a new body-size control.

07Rift occupation

  • Occupation gains the battlefield buff. Rejoining attackers receive temporary immunity and more arrival points near the active outposts.
  • Defenders receive attacked-outpost notices and the HUD Information button previews rewards.

08Interface

  • Raid/Transcendence lobbies show difficulty; quest-area mismatches across maps are fixed.

09Harvest login journey

  • The September 23–October 28 maintenance login event has all 28 daily rewards tabulated below.

19Quality of life

  • Some PvP amplification now applies to Abyss targets and occupation monoliths/guards; bosses/core gain slightly more HP, without specified ratios.
  • Dungeon retry retention rises 10→20 seconds, party visibility expands, ground targeting works over UI and chat scrolling stays in its panel.
  • Raid commendations extend from party to alliance, move right, sort by praise count and enable the pointer while open.
  • Arcana capacity is 1,100. Raid alliance parties can reorganize outside boss combat without moving the leader; ginseng tokens can use character/server storage.

20Other fixes

  • Qualified rift contribution grants boss rewards at distance. Name hiding now covers minimap lists, summons’ hit messages and kisk popups.
  • Fixes cover weekly point gauges, sold-out ticket prices showing free, the HUD dice button and map resolution when navigating to Abyss acquisition sources.
  • Commendation popups survive death/basic resurrection; selecting commendation info no longer closes the ranking character popup.
  • Fixes prevent gliding below Iskariel’s wipe and repair Deltras attacks at the basic resurrection point and direction controls after ropes.
  • Auto-potion slots display correctly after Nightmare; soul-imprinting probabilities show meaningful precision.

21Retroactive refunds

  • Logged September 16 bound-stone purchases receive the price difference in Nightmare/Festa currencies through the cube.
