FULL CHANGE LIST

Everything else in this patch

01Abyss

  • Artifact battles stay on Wednesday/Saturday, starting at 21:20, 21:50 and 22:20 for groups 1, 2 and 3.
  • If the core survives eight minutes, a weakening debuff reduces damage tolerance and strike resistance.
  • The three lower executors and three middle bosses appear Wednesday/Saturday at 21:45/22:15/22:45 by group.
  • Both Nahma variants appear Friday/Sunday at 22:00/22:30/23:00 by group. Weakening now also applies to enraged Nahma.

02Other fixes

  • Black-Smoke Murt’s Fire Temple jump landing matches its telegraph.
  • Triton’s final Cold Harvest breath cannot be nullified with immunity skills; affected skills no longer damage frozen targets.
  • The reduced Hidden Cube audio range is restored.
