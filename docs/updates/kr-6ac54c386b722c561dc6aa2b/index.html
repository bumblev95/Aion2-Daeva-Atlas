FULL CHANGE LIST

Everything else in this patch

01Achievements

  • World-map named bosses show an achievement badge only while an applicable kill achievement is incomplete.

02Nameplates

  • A new option hides your own nameplate under Information Display → General → Nameplate.

03Abyss

  • Exceeding the Upper Reshanta entry cap after entering causes immediate death, expulsion and a one-hour time deduction.
  • Rematching resets artifact and corridor ownership. The table lists all 21 pairings and their start times in the Korean notice’s timezone.
  • Large-battle performance is improved across upper, lower, middle and rift Abyss areas.

04Refining stones

  • Higher-grade stone odds rise in normal/hard Muspel’s Chalice and every Snowfield difficulty. The Expedition exchange adds 달인의 최상급 제련석 (bound).

05Abyss occupation

  • The faction without the final hit on the dimensional core also receives a reward.

06Wind Breeze Merchants

  • Bound resurrection stones and pet auto-loot tickets cost less. The ticket’s NPC resale value also falls; logged earlier purchases are due a retroactive Kina difference.

07Masterpiece remnants

  • Mode-specific Festa remnants become one common remnant; earlier items can be converted.
  • Expedition remnants gain Enhance Stone and Soul Crystal recipes. Logged discarded or sold remnants are due restoration; NPC sale proceeds are reclaimed first.

08Mission rewards

  • Verteron and Altgard mission rewards are brought in line with Eltnen and Morheim.

09Exchange-shop inventory

  • Abyss, Nightmare and Festa stores add Stigma Shards; the Abyss store adds a lower-AP Asphiel Daevanion Crystal offer.
  • Festa Soul Crystals become 50-unit bundles capped at 15 bundles weekly; Nightmare crystals also use 50-unit bundles.

10Daily-dungeon rewards

  • Low-score rewards increase, narrowing gaps between score bands.

11Hidden Cubes

  • Keyed Soul Crystal rewards rise from 100 to 1,000. Eligible Chapter 1 claims are due the retroactive difference.

12Resurrection event

  • October 7–21 maintenance window: an additional bound stone offer for level-45+ characters, 30 weekly per character at 25,000 Kina in the Special shop tab.

13End of the Expedition/Transcendence harvest event

  • Associated event items and recipes are removed, normal rewards resume and Hidden Cubes regain their standard appearance.

14Quality of life

  • A sound option toggles your own wing audio.
  • Equipment, wings, titles, pantheon, race understanding, skills, Arcana and HUD presets expand from 3 to 5; wardrobe presets from 3 to 6.

15Other fixes

  • Fixes cover invisible characters on Radeon 8060S, FSR artifacts, Redmi Note 14 startup crashes and cropped UI on some Galaxy Fold devices.
  • Placed world markers remain visible after moving within an alliance. The Fire Temple spirit-damage fix is included in the Spiritmaster card.
