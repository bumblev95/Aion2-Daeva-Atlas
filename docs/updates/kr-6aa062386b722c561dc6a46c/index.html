FULL CHANGE LIST

Everything else in this patch

01Sanctuary raids

  • The new Snowfield has easy/normal/hard modes. Entering a boss room also creates a starting-area portal to its entrance.

02Seal slot

  • A seal equipment slot arrives; seals come from crafting or Snowfield exchange tickets plus Kina.

03New gear

  • True dragon-king weapon/guard recipes and inheritance from shining variants are added; converted liberated-night gear supplies materials.

04Dungeon crafting materials

  • Seven draconic material types are redistributed. Krao, Draupnir, Urugugu, Vakron, Fire Temple, Fierce Horn, Dead Dramata and Cradle drop bound materials only.
  • The four later expeditions offer all seven types in bound and tradable forms; Muspel’s Chalice offers all seven bound types.

05Soul binding

  • A bound Truth philosopher’s stone is added; ten bound Power stones convert into one Truth stone.

06Abyss

  • Server rematching resets artifact/corridor ownership; the CM notice supplies the new pairings.

07Abyss rift

  • Boss stats increase and phase/boss counts change. Eight bosses gain up to two 25-second immunity phases below HP thresholds.
  • Rift bundles increase to offset fewer bosses, with new Abyss weekly/season tasks.

08Dimensional Invasion

  • The entry icon previews the encounter title/rewards before entering.

09Streamer mode

  • Other → Account adds separate account/name privacy toggles; hidden names use @플레이어 throughout the UI.

10Wardrobe

  • Pattern width and height can change independently; enabling aspect lock keeps the earlier proportional behavior.

11Inheritance crafting

  • Material gear must meet its required enhancement level.

12Commendations

  • After the last boss, choose a player and reason for one commendation per clear before the timed popup expires.
  • Eligible givers/receivers must remain in the party/dungeon and contribute sufficiently. At this date Sanctuary candidates are your own party; totals and related achievements are added.

13Sanctuary hall of fame

  • The first three cross-server hard Snowfield clears are recorded, excluding non-contributors, in the entry screen’s upper-left tab.
  • Records preserve names, server, legion, class, peak power/item level, alliance position, date and clear time despite later name changes.

14Rift occupation

  • A map occupation tab and notification-icon shortcut show the active battle.
  • Outposts per group fall 5→4 and battle time 25→15 minutes. Attackers need three captures; defenders need at least three protected outposts.
  • Flight consumption drops 50%; repeated same-player kills are limited and assist contribution decreases slightly.

24Quality of life

  • Seal-slot changes reset accessory quick-sale/storage settings. Memberships show the final 30 minutes and notify expiry, including at next login when expired offline.
  • Skill levels can differ by preset, resetting earlier presets 2/3. Chain-skill selection can alter the parent level; HUD editing adds a boss-mechanic gauge.

25Other fixes

  • List-style equipment slots and the server-transfer shop tab are removed. Disabled-chat mini UI and corridor timeout resurrection UI are fixed.
  • Fixes cover mutated Ulgorn’s camera, Attiel block/evasion handling, upscaler descriptions and opposing-faction monolith quest markers.
