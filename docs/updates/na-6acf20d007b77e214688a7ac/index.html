PATCH HIGHLIGHTS

Other patch highlights

01Shop during server transfers

  • Daily Dungeon instant tickets and Soul Crystals move from the Wind Breeze shop to its Special Substance Morph category.
  • Odyle Energy loses its 16-per-server weekly cap; the per-character weekly cap increases from 4 to 20. Lower Abyss Rift Stones (7-day) are sold for two weeks.

02Halloween attendance, missions and exchanges

  • Runs from maintenance October 13 PDT / 14 CEST until maintenance November 3 PST / 4 CET. Attendance awards 35 coins on ordinary days and 100 each on days 7 and 14.
  • NA missions reset weekly per server: five activity missions give 25 coins each, three logins give 30, and gear, scroll, invasion and Shugo missions give 20 each.
  • Town merchants exchange event coins. The special chest costs 250 coins and contains the chest skin and 10,000 Pet Soul Crystals. Odyle Energy costs 10 coins, with a weekly server cap of four and a level-45 requirement.
  • Expedition/Transcendence award Candy Key Fragments. Each colored key needs 60 fragments and has a weekly per-server limit of one. Red rewards include pet growth, yellow Abyss Points, and blue Odyle Energy.

03Abyss access and event

  • Conquerors of the Abyss runs between maintenance October 13 PDT / 14 CEST and October 27 PDT / 28 CET. Level-45 characters can buy up to seven Rift Stones weekly for 50,000 Kina each.
  • Abyss weekly missions for spending one hour and defeating Guardian Lord Nahma once gain entry-stone rewards; accumulated entry time is capped at seven hours.

04Cosmetic products

  • Adds armor and weapon skins, two pets, Spider Wings, and helm/decor items. The official tables provide prices, purchase limits and all components.

05Settings, performance and fixes

  • Reduces Abyss large-battle stutter, adds camera Y-axis inversion and own-HP-bar visibility options, and restricts gathering-material listings based on gameplay history.
  • Fixes dual-GPU settings, certain 4K refresh-rate crashes, FSR loading delays and Shugo reconnect position resets. Graphics settings stop syncing to Steam Cloud.
