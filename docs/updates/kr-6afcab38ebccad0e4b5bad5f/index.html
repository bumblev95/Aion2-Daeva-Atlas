PATCH NOTES / KR

November 18 · Korea

KR adjusts Gladiator, Templar, Assassin, Brawler, Chanter, Ranger and Cleric; adds Muspel Easy, Trust limits, free KR server transfer and PvP weapon stats; and fixes Hidden Cube, gathering and several boss-pattern issues. These November 18 rules stay KR-only and do not recalculate NA PvE, historical DPS or final tiers.

CLASS CHANGES

Gladiator ↑ Buff

Buff 1

Class guide →
  • ↑ Buff

    Ruinous Blow and Frenzy access

    Ruinous Blow cooldown changes 60→40s; specialization-3 charge timing changes 60/30→40/40s. Focused Block specialization 3 grants Frenzy for 10s instead of 5s, and Downward/Upward Strike threat increases 50%.

    Before60s cooldown; 60/30s charges; 5s Frenzy→After40s cooldown; 40/40s charges; 10s Frenzy

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: the core attack and Frenzy windows become more available, but total KR rotation DPS is not measured. Verify charge behavior, cooldown reduction, block trigger, threat and full rotation.

CLASS CHANGES

Templar ↔ Adjustment

Adjustment 1

Class guide →
  • ↔ Adjustment

    Judgment damage, cadence and setup

    Shield Smite now resets Judgment cooldown. Judgment cooldown changes 3→7s and base damage rises 50%, while specialization-3 consecutive bonuses change 50/100/150/200%→25/50/75/100%. Punishment loses its extra-damage condition tied to Judgment’s Authority.

    Before3s cooldown; 50/100/150/200% consecutive bonus→After7s cooldown; +50% base damage; 25/50/75/100% consecutive bonus

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: baseline hits rise while the natural cadence and repeat scaling fall; Shield Smite routing becomes central. Reproduce reset order, Authority state, threat and a repeatable rotation before eDPS use.

CLASS CHANGES

Assassin ↔ Adjustment

Buff 1 · Nerf 1

Class guide →
  • ↑ Buff

    Quick Slice chain PvE damage

    Quick Slice, Severing Slice and Swift Slice gain 10% PvE damage.

    BeforeCurrent PvE skill damage→After+10% relative PvE damage

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: the basic chain is stronger in KR PvE. This official skill-relative change is recorded without converting it into total class DPS.

  • ↓ Nerf

    Savage chain, Killing Intent and burst scaling

    Savage Roar/Kick PvE and PvP damage fall 20%. Killing Intent changes from a 3s +5% Crushing Blow effect to a 5s, three-stack +3% effect per stack; Heart Gore’s Killing Intent bonus changes 150→80%. Insignia Explosion cooldown improves 7→5s, but its per-insignia bonuses change 10/40/90/160/400%→10/30/60/100/250%.

    Before3s +5% Killing Intent; 150%; 7s; up to 400%→After5s, max 3 stacks at +3% each; 80%; 5s; up to 250%

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: sustained Killing Intent is easier to maintain and Explosion is faster, but peak Heart Gore, five-insignia and Savage-chain burst fall. Verify stacking refresh, Ambush multi-input fix, stack consumption and a complete KR burst loop.

CLASS CHANGES

Ranger ↑ Buff

Buff 1

Class guide →
  • ↑ Buff

    Precision routing and major PvE attacks

    Deadshot gains 15% PvE damage. Marking Shot grants Precision on use and Precision adds +5% all-element damage. Burst Arrow activates from Precision, gains 100% PvE and 30% PvP damage, and Resonance is granted on use; Arrow Scattershot likewise activates on use during Resonance. Snare Shot and Rooting Eye return to Slow/Root-oriented conditions.

    BeforeHit/CC-gated Precision and Burst Arrow→AfterUse-gated Precision/Resonance; +15% Deadshot; +100%/+30% Burst Arrow

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: the main burst becomes less dependent on hit timing and crowd-control state. Verify exact restored conditions, Precision/Resonance grant timing, removed conditional bonus, tooltips and rotation before KR eDPS use.

    Storm Order scaling and Spinning Strike

    Storm Order changes from weapon-damage amplification to Attack, gains +0.5s duration per skill level, changes specialization 3 from +2s duration to +10% weapon damage and specialization 4 from +7% to +10% Attack. Spinning Strike gains 10% PvE damage.

    BeforeWeapon amplification; +2s spec 3; +7% Attack spec 4→AfterAttack; +0.5s/level; +10% weapon damage spec 3; +10% Attack spec 4

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: buff duration and build choice change, while the personal attack skill rises. Verify level-by-level duration, party/personal scope, refresh, specialization combination and rotation; no party eDPS is inferred.

CLASS CHANGES

All classes ✓ Bug fix

Bug fix 2 · Other change 4

  • • Other change

    KR Trust limits and 05:00 reset

    A new account-level Trust system can apply daily integrated server/world-market settlement limits and exchange purchase limits. The available amount depends on account trust and resets daily at 05:00.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: trading and growth routes may be delayed for affected accounts. Verify account state, displayed limit, reset, settlement/purchase accounting and unlock conditions before adding fixed amounts.

  • • Other change

    KR free server transfer and merge rules

    KR server transfer opens free, while locked destination slots keep their existing unlock cost. Attendance, remaining cube rewards, Outland weekly rewards, Estate objects and Genus Insight follow published selection/merge rules.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: this affects KR migration planning only. Do not reopen or rewrite the existing Global transfer guide; verify eligibility, capacity, slot price, reward counters, Estate overflow deletion and Genus Insight selection in the KR client.

  • • Other change

    KR PvP weapon stats and ranking-point thresholds

    KR adds PvP Weapon Damage Amplification and PvP Weapon Damage Resistance. The Battleground Amulet’s PvP Damage Amplification is replaced by PvP Weapon Damage Amplification while breakthrough stats remain; baseline ranking points for Transcendence and Nightmare are reduced.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: KR PvP gearing comparisons and score targets may change, but no conversion formula or threshold values were published. Verify stat scope, stacking, old-item migration, tooltip values and the new Transcendence/Nightmare score thresholds before revising /gear/, /guides/pvp-context/ or /routines/.

  • • Other change

    Muspel Easy, reward conversions and dungeon close

    Muspel’s Chalice Easy is added. Selected guaranteed equipment-change tickets can be converted to a top-grade material choice box; additional pity-point conversion recipes are announced for November 25. After dungeon close, rewards cannot be obtained and automatic exit occurs after 10s.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: difficulty/reward and post-clear timing guidance may change. Verify entry gate, boss patterns, rewards, conversion costs/eligibility, November 25 rollout, close trigger and the 10s exit.

  • ✓ Bug fix

    Abyss Hidden Cube and gathering rules

    Fixes some Chaos Ereshuranta Middle hidden cubes spawning in hard-to-reach positions. Dungeon gathering no longer counts toward the server cap and remains available at the cap; repeated Abyss gathering cancellation now accumulates toward a temporary restriction.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: map and routine guidance needs client coordinates and counter tests. Preserve existing coordinates until spawn, altitude, acquisition condition, reset and cancellation counting are reproduced.

  • ✓ Bug fix

    Boss targeting, telegraphs and solo-pattern state

    Fixes Eracus Lifika not targeting the nearest player with Sandstorm, revived Auldor’s Whirling Calamity tornado moving outside its preview, Auldor/Beltiras not using solo patterns in solo play, and solo patterns persisting after another player joins.

    Combat impact P1 interpretation: current cue/response guidance can be wrong for these encounters. Verify difficulty, region, trigger, target selection, preview path, solo/party transition and correct response before revising animations or tactics.

CLASS CHANGES

Brawler · KR ↑ Buff

Buff 1

  • ↑ Buff

    Rage resource, sustain and cooldown flow

    Basic attacks restore Fury during Rage. Gale Strike specialization 1 changes from 15% life steal to 100 Fury; Chain Fist gains 0.5% life steal and specialization 1 adds 1%. Spinning Kick reset becomes 10% on block or 100% through Focused Block, and Typhoon Combo reduces Ascending Strike and Explosive Fist cooldowns by 1s on hit.
