October 6/7 · North America

Founder access across characters and servers, wing conversion limits, more leather and map, mission and control fixes.

FULL CHANGE LIST

Everything else in this patch

01Founder’s Pack

  • The tier-specific free shop supplies missing pack skins, titles and other items to characters across servers.
  • Access remains open for every character and server until a closure notice. Owned pack items cannot be claimed again.
  • Inventory duplicate disposal and duplicate skin registration fixes are planned for a later update.
  • Free duplicate skins do not consume paid-skin purchase limits. For incomplete pack purchases, the notice asks players to restart the client and retry.

02Items & substance morphing

  • Sealed Wings to Enhance Stones: at most 20 conversions per server each week.
  • Raw Leather drop rates increase in Chaotic Lower Reshanta, Verteron and Altgard. The percentage is unspecified.

03Wind Breeze Merchants · Season 1

  • The Special tab adds Resurrection Spiritstone (Season 1): 25,000 Kina each, up to 10 per server each week.
  • The notice gives a sale window from after October 6 maintenance (PDT) until before December 15 maintenance (PST). This uses the North America limit.

04Map & mission fixes

  • The map-pin maximum display is corrected; players can place 30 pins.
  • The Asmodian weekly Challenge Season mission Daeva of Glorious Deeds can complete correctly.

05Settings & chat fixes

  • Duplicate Movement Controls entries in Key Settings → General are removed.
  • Affected emote commands work correctly in chat.
