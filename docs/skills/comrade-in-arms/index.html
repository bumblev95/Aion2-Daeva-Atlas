Comrade in Arms

Increases the caster's PvE Damage Tolerance by 20% and PvP Damage Tolerance by 10% and redirects damage taken by a party member within 40m each time they are attacked, sharing 50% to the caster and 50% to the attacked party member for 6s. Unaffected by cooldown increase effects.

Effect at skill level 1

Increases the caster's PvE Damage Tolerance by 20% and PvP Damage Tolerance by 10% and redirects damage taken by a party member within 40m each time they are attacked, sharing 50% to the caster and 50% to the attacked party member for 6s. Unaffected by cooldown increase effects.

When to use it

Use the activation condition in the tooltip. Check range and resources before pressing.

Level milestones

  1. 1
    Lv. 5

    Restores 25% of Max HP once if hit during Comrade in Arms while HP is 25% or less

  2. 2
    Lv. 10

    x1.5 [Guarding Seal] Protective Shield effect for the duration

  3. 3
    Lv. 15

    x1.5 [Block Pain] effect for the duration

  4. 4
    Lv. 20

    Restores 50% of caster's Max HP when the effect ends

These effects accumulate at the listed levels; they are not competing choices.
