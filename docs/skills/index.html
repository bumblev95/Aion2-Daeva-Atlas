Know the skill behind the name.

English and Korean names, effects and practical usage.

17 key skills · Global DB English names · click for effects, usage and specializations.

Gladiator · ActiveLeaping Slam↗도약찍기

Leap to an enemy, deal damage and contribute to stagger. Prepare for Battle is a specialization, not the base skill.

Gladiator · ActiveRuinous Blow↗파멸의 맹타

A rushing area attack that also grants Prepare for Battle for 20 seconds.

Gladiator · StigmaZikel’s Blessing↗지켈의 축복

Temporarily increases your Attack and Accuracy. The stagger bonus requires a specialization.

Assassin · ActiveAmbush↗기습

A close attack with 30% more damage when it lands as a back attack.

Assassin · ActiveHeart Gore↗심장찌르기

Becomes available after a critical hit; damages nearby enemies and restores MP.

Chanter · StigmaPower of the Storm↗질풍의 권능

Gives combat speed and cooldown benefits to you and nearby allies. It conflicts with the Cleric’s Earth’s Blessing effect.

Chanter · ActiveImpactful Crush↗타격쇄

A ranged strike with a stun effect. It opens Dark Crush for two seconds.

Chanter · ActiveDark Crush↗암격쇄

A mobile ranged follow-up opened by Impactful Crush.

Cleric · ActiveBolt↗벽력

A wind area attack with up to three charge levels; more charging increases its damage and stagger contribution.

Sorcerer · ActiveHellfire↗지옥의 화염

A fire area attack with three charge levels. Casting while moving requires its movement specialization.

Spiritmaster · StigmaSummon: Ancient Spirit↗소환: 고대의 정령

Summons an Ancient Spirit for 30 seconds. Its skills grant Four Elements to the caster.

Spiritmaster · StigmaJointstrike: Destructive Attack↗협공: 파멸의 공세

You and your summoned spirit attack together. The spirit’s contribution depends on which spirit is active.

Spiritmaster · ActiveElemental Fusion↗원소융합

Spirit skills build elements. Four stacks enable this attack, which consumes Four Elements.

Spiritmaster · StigmaSeize Magic↗마법 강탈

An area attack that removes up to two buffs; removed buffs add damage.

Cleric · ActiveDefiance↗충격 해제

Removes listed control effects, including stun and airborne, and gives five seconds of status immunity.

Templar · StigmaTaunt↗도발

Deals damage and raises enmity. It also weakens the target’s Attack and Accuracy.

Ranger · ActiveSnipe↗저격

A ranged wind attack that restores MP.
