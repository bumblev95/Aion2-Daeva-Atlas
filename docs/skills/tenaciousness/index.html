Tenaciousness

The caster becomes immune to damage and status effects for 3s. Increases PvE Damage Tolerance by 50% and PvP Damage Tolerance by 25% for 3s when the effect ends. Cannot block certain powerful attacks. Unaffected by cooldown increase effects. Tenaciousness, Salvation, and Barrier Spell effects cannot be applied for 30s if Invincibility stacking is blocked.
