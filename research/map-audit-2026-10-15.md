# Map spatial and mobile review — October 15, 2026

Baseline: `151c688` (includes map PRs #36–39 and recovered official feed #40).
No marker positions, marker IDs, completion keys or region options are changed
by this review. The site remains a four-region map.

## Findings and changes

- **Confirmed mobile defect:** two-finger spread/close did not change scale.
  Track both pointers, keep the world position under their midpoint stable,
  rebase when a finger leaves, and handle cancellation. A gesture can begin
  over a marker; a drag must not activate a marker or cluster.
- **Confirmed shared-location defect:** the initial `ResizeObserver(fit)`
  notification overrode deep-link centering. Ignore unchanged initial size and
  preserve the viewed world centre on actual viewport resizing.
- **Coverage discrepancy, not a fabricated coordinate fix:** TH.GL publishes
  559 traces per growth region; the historical KR player guides describe 560.
  Show this limit when the trace/all filter is selected. The unresolved point
  is not invented, and differences in client/patch scope remain possible.

## Source and terrain checks

[Machine-readable evidence](map-spatial-audit.json) records hashes, source
settings, all-count checks, 47 deterministic browser anchors, independent
screenshot registrations and the unsupported-region inventory.

All 64 freshly fetched provider tiles match the previous review's hashes.
The four 2048×2048 local images preserve row/column order and the source's
positive axis transforms. Mean absolute channel differences from directly
joined source pixels are 0.48–0.60 out of 255, consistent with their WebP
recompression. No rotation, reflection, stretching or geographic shift was
found relative to TH.GL. Public terrain/JS/HTML/CSS snapshots (10 files) match
the baseline repository hashes.

All **9,738 imported positions**, including the later gathering update, match
source positions. The six older Verteron travel IDs remain stable while their
coordinates match the provider's newer IDs. Target categories include 1,118
trace positions, 2,811 cube candidates, 122 Sealed Dungeons and 126 travel
points across the four supported regions. The tutorial regions have no
source traces/cubes/Sealed Dungeons; they are not populated by inference.

## Separate game-image corroboration

Used player-created captures, not just our generated map:

- [섬댕이's Verteron map, November 2025](https://www.inven.co.kr/board/aion2/6444/316).
- [시즈모드's Altgard routes, December 2025](https://www.inven.co.kr/board/aion2/6444/600),
  specifically the Unnamed Graveyard and Spirit Island captures.

Terrain feature registration finds positive uniform scales and effectively
zero rotation: 2,858 inliers in the Verteron overview, 35 and 30 in the two
Altgard crops. Median residuals are 0.31, 1.21 and 1.52 pixels in the downscaled
references. These are **terrain registration residuals**, not per-marker
accuracy measurements. North/east/south/west features and surrounding land,
lakes, coasts and islands are consistent.

Projected trace/travel/dungeon samples were visually compared with the
captures. Eight numbered Altgard traces and visible travel/dungeon icons
provide useful corroboration. One dungeon is obscured by the author's title;
small Verteron feather icons are not unambiguous at overview scale. These are
recorded as unresolved rather than labelled correct. Historical screenshots
do not verify today's client, Z access, or live objects. No independent game
capture confirms the hidden-cube candidates or the two tutorial maps in this
review; their checked positions are provider-relative only. Publisher/player
images were used transiently for review and are not republished in this repo.

## Mobile behavior and regression protection

`tests/map-spatial-ui.test.js` checks all 47 independently derived expected
coordinates in EN/KR, rendered marker centering, source-link coordinates,
completion across reload/language change, and 320/390px touch gestures:
spread/close, stable midpoint, subsequent single-finger pan, cancellation, marker taps and
zoom controls. The existing map test still covers material filters, candidate
counts, clustering, pagination, faction context and empty tutorial recovery.
The browser workflow runs both and retains screenshots.

Local browser tests use Chromium with touch emulation; this is not a physical
phone test. Direct public Chromium navigation was blocked by this executor's
network/TLS setup. Public assets were fetched with validated TLS, checked by
hash, and the matching code tested locally. No certificate warning was bypassed.

To replay source matching without networking during normal builds:

```sh
# Place fetched, reviewed .raw node files in CACHE, named by their map keys.
# Extract tilesConfig from the independently fetched provider page's RSC JSON.
npm install --prefix /tmp/map-review cbor-x --no-save --package-lock=false
NODE_PATH=/tmp/map-review/node_modules node scripts/verify_map_source_snapshot.js CACHE PROVIDER_TILES_JSON
node tests/world-map-ui.test.js
node tests/map-spatial-ui.test.js
```

eligibility need review before these regions are added. No new regions or
unverified points were published.
