## Verification story

Readers enter through a goal menu or search, open a dated regional guide,
choose one topic, inspect its original diagram or numerical example, switch
languages with the topic preserved, and follow the reward/material source.
All substantive text also remains available through native disclosures
without JavaScript.

The advanced-guides UI test verifies six routes in both languages at
320, 390, 768 and 1440 pixels; menus, search and language links; blank,
negative, decimal and excessive numerical inputs; same-step compensation;
Stigma reverse-target handling; visible autoplay and pause; and the
no-script fallback. The browser CI retains screenshots.
